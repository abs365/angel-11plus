(b) 84 ÷ ___ = 7. What number belongs in the blank?

**Question 3** *(3 marks)*
(a) A recipe needs 750ml of milk per cake. How many litres of milk are needed for 6 cakes?
(b) A fence is built from 6 identical wooden planks, each 85cm long, placed end to end. What is the total length of the fence, in metres?
(c) A box contains 8 identical bags of flour, each weighing 375g. What is the total weight of the box's contents, in kilograms?

**Question 4** *(2 marks)*
(a) A function machine multiplies a number by 3, then adds 4. What is the output when the input is 7?
(b) A function machine multiplies a number by 5, then subtracts 6. What is the output when the input is 9?

**Question 5** *(2 marks)*
(a) A shop increases the price of an £80 jacket by 25%, then reduces the new price by 20% in a sale. What is the final price, in pounds?
(b) A laptop originally costs £600. Its price is reduced by 15% in a sale, then a loyalty discount reduces the sale price by a further 10%. What is the final price, in pounds?

**Question 6** *(2 marks)*
(a) In a triangle, the three angles are x degrees, (x + 10) degrees, and (x + 20) degrees. What is the value of x?
(b) In a quadrilateral, the four angles are y degrees, y degrees, (y + 30) degrees, and (y + 30) degrees. What is the value of y?

**Question 7** *(4 marks)*
*A camping shop sells tents.*
(a) One tent has an original price of £120. For a summer sale, the shop reduces this tent's price by 15%. What is the tent's price during the summer sale?
(b) A tent that was reduced by 15% to £102 is then given a further 10% off for loyalty-card customers. What does the loyalty-card customer pay for the tent?
(c) Some customers think a 15% discount followed by a 10% discount is the same as a single 25% discount. A tent originally priced at £120 actually costs £91.80 after the two discounts are applied in order. How much MORE is this than the price a single 25% discount straight off the £120 original price would give?
(d) A different tent is sold after a single 20% discount is applied to its own original price, making its sale price £136. What was that tent's original price before the discount?

**Question 8** *(2 marks)*
A tailor starts making a costume at 14:20. Sewing takes 1 hour 50 minutes, and finishing touches take a further 25 minutes.
(a) What time is the costume finished? Give your answer in 24-hour time.
(b) Each costume uses 2.5 metres of fabric costing £4.80 per metre. What is the cost of fabric for one costume?

**Question 9** *(3 marks)*
*A collector has three bags of marbles: red, blue and green. The blue bag has 6 more marbles than the red bag. The green bag has 3 times as many marbles as the blue bag. Altogether, the three bags contain 64 marbles.*
(a) How many marbles are in the red bag?
(b) How many marbles are in the green bag?
(c) How many more marbles are in the green bag than in the red and blue bags combined?

**Question 10** *(2 marks)*
(a) A function machine multiplies a number by 3, then adds 4. The output is 31. What was the input?
(b) A function machine multiplies a number by 4, then subtracts 7. The output is 41. What was the input?

**Question 11** *(2 marks)*
A school running club recorded how many pupils attended each week during one half-term.

| Week | Attendance |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | 14 |
| Week 2 | 19 |
| Week 3 | 16 |
| Week 4 | 23 |
| Week 5 | 21 |

(a) Each week, the club charges £1.50 per attending pupil for a hot drink afterwards. How much money was collected in total across all five weeks, in pounds?
(b) Between which two consecutive weeks did attendance increase by the greatest amount? Give your answer in the form "Week X to Week Y".

**Question 12** *(2 marks)*
(a) After a 20% discount, a jacket costs £52. What was the original price, in pounds?
(b) A shop increases all its prices by 15%. A bike now costs £115. What was its price before the increase, in pounds?

**Question 13** *(4 marks)*
*At the Oakwood Athletics Meet, the number of adult spectators rounds to 380 to the nearest 10. The number of child spectators rounds to 240 to the nearest 10.*
(a) What is the largest possible number of adult spectators?
(b) What is the smallest possible number of child spectators?
(c) What is the largest possible total number of spectators, adults and children added together?
(d) What is the smallest possible value of the number of adult spectators minus the number of child spectators?

**Question 14** *(2 marks)*
(a) An isosceles triangle has an apex angle of 38 degrees (the angle between the two equal sides). What is the size of each base angle, in degrees?
(b) An isosceles triangle has a base angle of 63 degrees. The apex angle (the angle between the two equal sides) is then split exactly in half by a line of symmetry. What is the size of each half, in degrees?

**Question 15** *(4 marks)*
*Riverside Primary School held a sponsored fun run. The table below shows how many laps each runner completed.*

| Laps completed | Number of runners |
|---|---|
| 0 | 3 |
| 1 | 5 |
| 2 | 8 |
| 3 | 6 |
| 4 | 5 |
| 5 | 3 |

(a) How many runners took part in the fun run in total?
(b) What was the total number of laps completed by all runners combined?
(c) What was the mean (average) number of laps completed per runner? Give your answer to 1 decimal place.
(d) A certificate is awarded to any runner who completed more laps than the mean (average) number of laps per runner. How many runners received a certificate?

**Question 16** *(3 marks)*
(a) ___ × 7 − 18 = 31. What number belongs in the blank?
(b) ___ × 5 + 12 = 62. What number belongs in the blank?
(c) ___ × 9 − 24 = 30. What number belongs in the blank?

**Question 17** *(3 marks)*
*A number puzzle uses a hidden positive whole number, n. Three other values are defined by these rules: P = n + 9, Q = 9 × n, and R = n × n.*
(a) What is the value of 9P − Q?
(b) What is the value of Q ÷ (P − 9)?
(c) What is the value of (P × n) − Q − R?

**Question 18** *(4 marks)*
*A bus company runs a route from Hillview to Oakford, stopping at Milltown and Riverside. The timetable below shows the morning and afternoon journey times.*

| Stop | Morning | Afternoon |
|---|---|---|
| Hillview | 08:00 | 14:15 |
| Milltown | 08:40 | 14:50 |
| Riverside | 09:05 | 15:22 |
| Oakford | 09:35 | 15:50 |

(a) How many minutes does the morning bus take to travel from Hillview to Oakford?
(b) How many minutes longer does the afternoon bus take from Milltown to Riverside than the morning bus takes for the same stage?
(c) Someone takes the morning bus to Milltown, then waits, then takes the afternoon bus onward from Milltown. How many minutes could they spend at Milltown?
(d) The bus company plans to reduce the afternoon Hillview-to-Milltown journey time by 20%. How many minutes should the new journey take?

**Question 19** *(2 marks)*
(a) A rope is cut into two pieces. One piece is 3 times as long as the other. Together the two pieces measure 48 metres. How long is the shorter piece, in metres?
(b) Two tanks together hold 90 litres of water. The larger tank holds 4 times as much as the smaller tank. How many litres does the larger tank hold?

**Question 20** *(2 marks)*
A group starts making bunting at 10:15. They work for 1 hour 40 minutes, then take a 15-minute tea break, then work for a further 55 minutes.
(a) What time do they finish? Give your answer in 24-hour time.
(b) Each metre of bunting uses 6 triangular flags, and 3.5 metres of bunting are made. Each flag costs £0.35 to make. What is the total cost of the flags?

**Question 21** *(3 marks)*
*A craft fair stall sells keyrings, bracelets and stickers. Keyrings are sold in packs of 5 for £2.00 each pack. Bracelets are sold individually for £1.20 each. Stickers are sold in packs of 8 for £1.60 each pack.*

| Item | Pack size | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Keyrings | Pack of 5 | £2.00 |
| Bracelets | Sold individually | £1.20 |
| Stickers | Pack of 8 | £1.60 |

(a) What is the total cost of 15 keyrings, 6 bracelets and 24 stickers? Give your answer in pounds, using 2 decimal places.
(b) If someone wanted to buy 40 of the same item, which item would work out cheapest?
(c) A customer spent exactly £8.40 buying only bracelets and stickers, with at least one of each. How many bracelets did they buy?
