## 2. What a validator can do in each pack

- Read each question with its stimulus (passage, table or diagram), and type **their own answer before the reveal** (the intended answer, marking treatment and alternatives sit behind a closed disclosure, below the own-answer box).
- Inspect the intended answer, the marking contract (automatic or trained marker, accepted alternatives, plausible wrong answers, coordinate and number rules) and, in Mathematics, the worked method.
- Record **structured checks** (OK, Concern or n/a, plus a free-text reason): English per question (answer correct, evidence in passage, demand fair, marks fair, unambiguous with complete alternatives, marking treatment suits); per passage (authenticity, age suitability, demand, vocabulary, evidence spread; factual claims for The Great Stink); whole section (skill coverage, mark distribution, passage variety, difficulty balance, marking mix). Mathematics per item (solved independently and unique, wording, answer format, difficulty label too easy / about right / too hard, CSSE-style plausibility, figure or table clarity); whole paper (coverage, difficulty spread, near-duplicates, overall authenticity, answer formats).
- Record **APPROVE, REVISE or REJECT** per item with a reason (required for REVISE and REJECT), and age suitability.
- **Export** a CSV and a JSON file. Both carry the pack name, the pack fingerprint, the reviewer's name and capacity (external educator, the Founder, or other), and one row or record per question identifier (the identifiers are never changed).

Writing is clearly separated in the English pack by a status table: **existing approved content** (Q1, approved for human validation, not activated); **assessment specification** for review (Q2 task and picture requirements); **provisional storyboard only** (the Q2 drawing, not approved artwork); **final Q2 production artwork: NOT READY**; and the **other 7 pending Writing prompts: not part of this pack**.

## 6. How to distribute the packs

1. Choose reviewers who did **not** write, generate or previously review the items. The reviewers' independence declaration is part of the pack; for Mathematics and English, at least one qualified independent educator per pack (the Founder to decide whether more are wanted).
2. The packs contain **intended answers**, so send them only to the chosen reviewer, by a secure route (not a public link, not a shared drive open to the school, not a family account). Send the single HTML file and `README.txt`.
3. Tell the reviewer: open the file in a normal browser on a laptop or tablet; work the questions before opening the answers; use the same browser and device throughout; press **Export my decisions** when done and return **both** the CSV and the JSON by the same secure route. If anything is unclear, record it in the comments rather than guessing.
4. On return, store the two files unmodified, record their SHA256, and check that the pack fingerprint inside matches the pack that was sent (above). Do not edit a returned file.
5. Attribution: the Founder's own decisions, if recorded in the same pack, are exported under capacity "The Founder" and are kept distinct from external educators. Claude must not fill in, approve or alter any reviewer decision, and does not count as a reviewer.
