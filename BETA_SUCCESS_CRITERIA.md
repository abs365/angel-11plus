# Angel 11+ Beta Success Criteria

## Overview

This document defines the success criteria for each stage of the beta programme.
Criteria should be evaluated **weekly** by the founder. Progress against these targets
drives product and growth decisions before any monetisation is introduced.

---

## Stage 1 — First 10 Families

**Target date:** Within 4 weeks of Phase 3 launch

### Engagement

- [ ] ≥ 7 of 10 families complete at least 3 sessions in their first week
- [ ] ≥ 5 of 10 families select an exam pathway within their first visit
- [ ] ≥ 5 of 10 families visit the Parent Dashboard at least once
- [ ] Average streak across all families: ≥ 3 days

### Retention

- [ ] ≥ 6 of 10 families still active after 2 weeks
- [ ] ≥ 4 of 10 families still active after 4 weeks
- [ ] At least 1 family attempts a full mock exam

### Feedback Quality

- [ ] ≥ 5 feedback submissions received (any type)
- [ ] ≥ 2 bug reports received and investigated
- [ ] ≥ 3 feature requests received
- [ ] At least 1 family conversation (email or call) completed by founder

### Testimonials

- [ ] ≥ 3 testimonials collected
- [ ] ≥ 2 testimonials with publish permission

### Critical Blockers

Any of the following should trigger an immediate fix before recruiting more families:

- A family cannot choose a pathway without help
- A family does not understand the Parent Dashboard
- A mock exam fails to complete and save results
- Dark mode or mobile layout is broken for any family

---

## Stage 2 — First 25 Families

**Target:** After Stage 1 success criteria met

### Engagement

- [ ] ≥ 60% of families active in week 2
- [ ] ≥ 40% of families active in week 4
- [ ] ≥ 10 mock exams completed across all families
- [ ] At least 2 families complete mocks across 2+ different pathways
- [ ] Average sessions per active family per week: ≥ 4

### Retention

- [ ] ≥ 50% of families still active after 1 month
- [ ] ≥ 3 families reaching a 7-day streak
- [ ] ≥ 1 family reaching a 14-day streak

### Feedback Quality

- [ ] ≥ 15 total feedback submissions
- [ ] ≥ 5 bug reports investigated and resolved
- [ ] ≥ 10 feature requests — pattern analysis completed
- [ ] Top 3 feature requests identified and added to roadmap

### Testimonials

- [ ] ≥ 8 testimonials collected
- [ ] ≥ 5 testimonials with publish permission
- [ ] At least 1 testimonial referencing a specific subject or pathway

### Product Milestones

- [ ] Zero critical bugs open longer than 48 hours
- [ ] Parent Dashboard shows meaningful data for all active families
- [ ] Getting Started guide used by ≥ 50% of new families (track via contact/support requests)

---

## Stage 3 — First 50 Families

**Target:** After Stage 2 success criteria met

### Engagement

- [ ] ≥ 60% of families active after 1 month
- [ ] ≥ 25 mock exams completed total
- [ ] ≥ 10 families have completed mocks in their target pathway
- [ ] Reading comprehension lessons: ≥ 50 total sessions

### Retention

- [ ] ≥ 40% of families still active after 6 weeks
- [ ] ≥ 5 families reaching a 14-day streak
- [ ] Churn rate (families gone completely inactive) < 30% in first month

### Pathway Distribution

- [ ] At least 3 of the 4 main pathways (GL, CEM, CSSE, ISEB) are represented
- [ ] At least 1 family in each year group (Y4, Y5, Y6)

### Feedback Quality

- [ ] ≥ 30 total feedback submissions
- [ ] Feature gap analysis complete — top 5 features prioritised
- [ ] NPS proxy: testimonials are predominantly positive (≥ 80%)
- [ ] Founder has spoken directly to ≥ 10 families

### Testimonials

- [ ] ≥ 15 testimonials collected
- [ ] ≥ 10 testimonials with publish permission
- [ ] Testimonials cover at least 3 different subjects or pathways

### Readiness Gate for Subscriptions

All of the following must be true before subscriptions are introduced:

- [ ] ≥ 40 of 50 families active for more than 4 weeks
- [ ] Fewer than 3 critical bugs reported in the previous 2 weeks
- [ ] At least 10 families have explicitly said they would pay for Angel 11+
- [ ] Pricing model validated with at least 5 family interviews
- [ ] No founder-assistance required for onboarding (confirmed via feedback)

---

## Evaluation Cadence

| Frequency | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Daily | Check admin dashboard; resolve any critical bugs |
| Weekly | Review engagement, feedback, and feature requests |
| Bi-weekly | Founder conversation with at least 1 beta family |
| Monthly | Full stage review against criteria above |

---

## What Success Looks Like

> "A parent we have never spoken to can discover Angel 11+, understand it, start their
> child on the correct pathway, use it for two weeks, and tell another parent about it —
> without any help from us."

When that is consistently true, Phase 3 is complete.
