# Release 1 — Multi-Year Question Pattern Analysis

**Programme:** Angel Assessment Transformation Execution Programme — Release 1
**Prepared:** 2026-08-10
**Status:** Analysis only. Synthesises and organises evidence already established by AEP-002/AEP-004 around the specific lens the Founder requested — recurring pattern vs. legitimate variation vs. single-year observation — rather than re-deriving it. Cross-checked directly against a fresh reading of the 2022 English and Mathematics papers (CSSE-008, CSSE-011).

**Evidence scope:** three entry years only (2021, 2022, 2023 — the only years CSSE has published papers for). Every claim below is a pattern across exactly three data points, and is labelled accordingly. No claim in this document should be read as describing CSSE's assessment design beyond what three consecutive years can show — the same discipline AEP-002 §7 already applies.

---

## 1. Stable Across All Three Years (highest confidence — safe to treat as structural rules)

| Characteristic | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Two separate tests (English, Mathematics), same day, 50/50 weighted, age-standardised | AEP-002 Obs. 1 (CSSE-001) |
| English = single 70-minute sitting, 3 internal sections (Comprehension 30+10 reading, Applied Reasoning 10, Continuous Writing 20) | AEP-002 Obs. 2, cross-validated CSSE-001 + CSSE-003/008/013 cover pages — this analysis independently confirmed CSSE-008's cover page states the identical breakdown |
| Mathematics = single 60-minute, 60-mark paper, calculators prohibited, exact-match marking only (no partial/method credit) | AEP-002 Obs. 3 |
| Comprehension passages are narrative fiction extracts from real, identifiable published novels | AEP-002 Obs. 6 (2021: *A Traveller in Time*; 2022: *The Good Companions*; 2023: *I Capture the Castle*) |
| Comprehension mark schemes explicitly instruct flexible/paraphrase acceptance of correct answers | AEP-002 Obs. 7 |
| Applied Reasoning is always a 5-question, 5-mark, 10-minute letter/word-code puzzle | AEP-002 Obs. 5 |
| Continuous Writing requires exactly 2 prompts, minimum "at least six sentences" each | AEP-002 Obs. 8 |
| Continuous Writing Question 1 is always reflective/discursive; Question 2 is always the identical instruction "Write a story based on the picture below" | AEP-004 §2 finding 3 |
| Mathematics paper spans 5 distinct domains within one paper (arithmetic, algebra, geometry, word problems, number theory) | AEP-002 Obs. 11, confirmed to full depth in all 3 years by AEP-004 |
| Mathematics questions are frequently compound (one numbered question stacking 2+ Question Types) | AEP-004 §6, directly re-confirmed this pass: CSSE-011 Q4 stacks unit conversion (QT-MR-03) with a further calculation |
| Comprehension's opening question is always a tick-box Yes/No judgement plus written justification (QT-RC-02) | AEP-004, re-confirmed this pass: CSSE-008 Q1 identical shape to CSSE-003 Q2/CSSE-013 Q1 |

## 2. Legitimate Variation (recurring genre, changing specific instance — not evidence of inconsistency)

| What varies | What stays fixed | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Applied Reasoning's specific task mechanic (2021: shared letter-pairs, same meaning; 2022: shared letter-pairs, reversed; 2023: alphabet-position numeric cipher) | The letter/word-code puzzle genre, 5Q/5 marks/10 min | AEP-002 Obs. 5 |
| Continuous Writing prompt topics (2021: favourite building; 2022: food and mood; 2023: favourite relaxing place) | Two-prompt structure, minimum-length instruction, fixed Q1/Q2 ordering | AEP-002 Obs. 8; AEP-004 §2 |
| Mathematics coordinate-task mechanic (2021: complete collinear points; 2022: rotation; 2023: plot-and-join) | The general coordinate/transformation reasoning demand (QT-MR-08) | AEP-004 |
| Data-representation type (2021/2022: bar chart; 2023: table) | The extract-then-compute demand (QT-MR-09) | AEP-004; re-confirmed this pass, CSSE-011 Q15 bar chart |
| Reading Comprehension passage genre's specific book/author each year | All three are narrative fiction (no non-fiction/poetry observed) | AEP-002 Obs. 6 — genre itself could still be a 3-data-point coincidence, flagged in Section 4 |
| Number-property reasoning sub-format weighting (true/false judgement present 3/3 years; property-satisfying search present 2023 only) | The number-property competency (MR-05) overall | AEP-004, QT-MR-11 |

## 3. Changes Over Time (directional, not yet confirmed as a sustained trend)

| Change | Years | Confidence | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Continuous Writing total marks: 15 → 15 → 20 | 2021, 2022, 2023 | MEDIUM — 2 agreeing, 1 differing; this analysis does not assert the 2023 change is permanent | AEP-002 Obs. 9 |
| Continuous Writing scoring structure: undifferentiated marker box → undifferentiated → explicit Content(15)/SPAG(5) split | 2021, 2022, 2023 | MEDIUM — single-year introduction | AEP-002 Obs. 13 |
| CSSE total consortium membership: 10 schools → 9 schools (unrelated to question content, carried here only because it is another "changes over time, don't assume stability" example from the same evidence programme) | 2025 Entry Guide, 2026 Entry Guide | HIGH for the fact itself | `RELEASE_0_EXECUTION_REPORT.md` Item 2 (cross-referenced, not re-derived) |

**This analysis explicitly does not extrapolate a trend line from two-or-three data points** (e.g. "marks are increasing" or "scoring is becoming more granular") — each change above is recorded as an observed shift between specific years, not a predicted trajectory.

## 4. Single-Year-Only Observations (do not treat as permanent rules)

Per AEP-004's own EMC-1/EMC-2 flags, the following are real but observed in exactly one of the three years — naming them here as a group so this analysis's own reader does not have to cross-reference the full 27-type catalogue to find them:

- QT-RC-03 (Word/Phrase Meaning-in-Context Explanation, standalone open form) — 2023 only (CSSE-003 Q4)
- QT-RC-06 (Sequential Ordering) — 2023 only (CSSE-003 Q3)
- QT-RC-09 (Multi-Select Tick-Box) — 2021 only (CSSE-013 Q11) — the only closed-response Comprehension format found in any year
- QT-MR-02 (Missing-Operand Arithmetic) — confirmed 2021 and 2023, not located in 2022's early questions as reviewed
- QT-MR-11's property-satisfying-search sub-format — 2023 only

**Why this matters for authoring:** per Gate 1's own tier structure, these EMC-1/EMC-2 types sit in Tier 4 (structurally-hard gaps), specifically *because* single-instance evidence cannot yet support confident authoring guidance — this analysis does not manufacture a stronger pattern than three data points (or fewer) can bear.

## 5. Comparison Method

For each Question Type in AEP-004's catalogue, this analysis checked: (a) does the underlying format appear in all three years available? (b) if it varies, does the *genre* stay fixed while the *specific instance* changes (Section 2), or does the *measured quantity itself* change (Section 3)? (c) is it observed in only one year, and if so, is that because the paper's own content varies naturally (expected) or because the format may not be a genuine CSSE convention at all (cannot be determined from this evidence base)? No comparison asserts consistency the underlying papers do not show — where 2022's specific data point could not be verified by this analysis's own tooling (AEP-002 Obs. 4, the unreadable 2022 combined-marks total), that gap is carried forward as unresolved, not filled by inference from the other two years.
