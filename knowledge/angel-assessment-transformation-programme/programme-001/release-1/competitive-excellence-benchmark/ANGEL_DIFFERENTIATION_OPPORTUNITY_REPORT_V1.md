## Angel's Genuine, Already-Real Advantages (Verified, Not Claimed)

Per the governing instruction's explicit caution — **an architectural capability is not a commercial claim until implemented and validated** — the following are stated only because they were independently re-verified against the actual repository this session, not merely asserted:

1. **Competency-level, evidence-gated diagnosis** (`computeEducationalState()`, real, live) — none of the three competitors observed this deeply; Atom and Think Academy's "progress reports" appear to be aggregate/subject-level, not competency-level with a confidence gate.
2. **Wellbeing veto as a structural, always-on mechanism** — no competitor observed has an equivalent; this is a genuine, real, differentiated safeguard.
3. **Audience-aware, fact-grounded explainability** — `generateExplanation()` produces parent- and learner-specific text from real computed evidence, not a human-authored template. Think Academy's reports are presumably human-authored (a different, not necessarily lesser, mechanism) — the distinction is real and worth stating plainly, not claiming superiority without qualification.
4. **Durable mastery with a genuine 14-day, evidence-gated maintenance cycle** — no competitor observed shows anything resembling this level of mechanism.
5. **The "no forced fit" / evidence-traceability discipline this entire programme has held** — the Essex Tutors' "original content" claim is the closest competitor analogue observed, but Angel's chain (CSSE Asset ID → Question Type → competency → original item → review → eligibility) is more rigorous than anything observed on any competitor's public-facing pages.

**None of these are commercial claims yet** — per instruction, they remain internal architectural facts until the choice-injection point and family-choice layer (the one clear gap, per the Capability Matrix) are actually built and validated.

## What Angel Should Deliberately NOT Copy

- Atom's raw "standardised age score" framing — conflicts with Angel's own wellbeing-first design.
- Think Academy's live-tutor-centric model as a wholesale strategy — different business, different cost structure; Angel's proposition is evidence-led personalisation at digital scale, not tutor replacement.
- Any provider's exact pricing structure, sight unseen of Angel's own cost base — explicitly deferred, not decided here.
- Essex Tutors' physical-venue model — a different product category, not a gap in Angel's digital platform.
