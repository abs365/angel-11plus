# Continuous Writing Competitive Review V1

**Programme:** Angel Assessment Transformation Execution Programme — Release 1
**Prepared:** 2026-08-11

---

## What Was Actually Observed

**Across all three providers researched this session, no Continuous-Writing-specific feature, page, or claim was surfaced** in the pages reached — not teaching, not planning, not automated feedback, not human marking, not timed-writing practice. This is recorded honestly as an absence in what was observed, not proof the feature doesn't exist elsewhere on each site (NOT VERIFIED beyond the pages reached this pass) — English/writing appears folded generically into "English" subject coverage on Atom's pricing tiers, and was not a focus of either the Essex Tutors or Think Academy pages reached.

## Implication for Angel

This is a genuine, if narrow, potential differentiation space — **if** Angel's own Continuous Writing capability is built credibly, which per the standing evidence finding it is not yet. Two real constraints must not be conflated:

1. **Competitor absence does not resolve Angel's own evidence gap.** The Continuous Writing Evidence Review already found the official CSSE marking rubric insufficient for a defensible numeric score — that finding is unchanged by what competitors do or don't offer. **Competitor practice may inform product design (e.g. how a teaching sequence is structured) but cannot substitute for or resolve an official-evidence gap**, exactly as the governing instruction states.
2. **Angel's own currently-live writing-feedback endpoint already has an unsupported, invented CSSE-attributed scoring model** (found and recorded in `CONTINUOUS_WRITING_EVIDENCE_REVIEW_V1.md` §9a). Before any competitive claim about Continuous Writing could be made, this pre-existing internal defect would need addressing — a fact entirely internal to Angel, unrelated to competitor research, worth restating here so it isn't lost in a competitive-framing document.

## What Would Be Needed Before Any Competitive Claim

Per the Excellence Model's own TEACHING/PRACTICE/ASSESSMENT split: Angel could credibly claim differentiation on the **teaching and practice** stages (task-authentic prompts, targeted skill-building, dimension-level diagnosis within the 5 evidenced rubric criteria) without needing the assessment/scoring layer resolved first — since those stages are evidence-sufficient today. Any claim resting on **scoring accuracy or CSSE-mark equivalence** must wait for the evidence gate, regardless of what competitors do or don't do.

## Recommendation

Do not build competitive messaging around Continuous Writing yet. If a future increment implements the Excellence Model's teaching/practice stages (leaving assessment explicitly evidence-gated and disclosed), that is a legitimate, evidence-grounded basis for differentiation — building it to match a competitor's marketing, or in the scoring layer specifically, is not.
