# Founder Decision Report — Competitive Excellence Benchmark

**Programme:** Angel Assessment Transformation Execution Programme — Release 1
**Prepared:** 2026-08-11
**Status:** Competitive analysis complete. Nothing implemented. Answers below are direct.

---

### A. What can parents currently obtain from these competitors?

From Atom Learning: a self-serve digital subscription (£39.99-£69.99/mo) with practice papers, structured learning across subjects, and — at the top tier only — unlimited mock tests with automated marking. From 11+ Tutors in Essex: real, invigilated, CSSE-and-CCHS/FSCE-specific mock exam sittings at a physical venue, with cohort ranking and postal feedback within 3 days. From Think Academy: live tutor-led classes (online and in-person), Maths-focused for 11+/13+, with per-lesson feedback reports and post-mock analysis sessions.

### B. Where are competitors demonstrably stronger than today's Angel?

Essex Tutors: genuine CSSE-specificity stated explicitly, and real cross-learner cohort ranking — Angel has neither today. Think Academy: live human teaching and a relationship-based feedback ritual — Angel has no human-tutor layer at all, by design. Atom: sheer claimed mock-volume ("unlimited fresh") — Angel's real pool (29 items) is far behind whatever volume Atom's claim represents.

### C. Where does Angel already possess stronger underlying capability?

Competency-level evidence-gated diagnosis, a structural wellbeing veto, audience-aware fact-grounded explainability, and durable-mastery maintenance tracking — all real, verified against the repository this session, none observed at comparable depth in any of the three competitors researched. See Register A1-A5.

### D. What capabilities must Angel genuinely build or strengthen?

The choice-injection/family-choice mechanism (the one clear architectural gap blocking the whole approved personalisation direction), question-bank depth toward the fresh-mock readiness floor, a real server-side Mock Attempt Ledger, and — if cohort benchmarking is pursued — a genuinely new capability with no existing analogue today.

### E. What should Angel deliberately NOT copy?

Raw "standardised age score" framing (conflicts with Angel's own wellbeing-first, qualitative-readiness design); Think Academy's live-tutor-centric model as a wholesale strategy (a different business); any provider's pricing structure adopted sight-unseen of Angel's own cost base.

### F. What would make Angel educationally distinctive?

The combination no competitor observed offers together: CSSE-specific evidence-derived authenticity (not just "11+" generically), competency-level diagnosis with explainability, family choice genuinely respected alongside system recommendation, and mastery maintenance that prevents "focus" from becoming "forgetting." Individually, competitors match pieces of this; none combines them.

### G. What must exist before Angel can credibly offer unlimited/fresh CSSE mocks?

A directional floor of roughly 150-250 Independently Validated items (not a firm target, an honesty floor), evidence-priority-matched distribution rather than concentration in already-covered types, and a real duplication check — none of which exists today. See `REPEATED_FRESH_MOCK_READINESS_MODEL_V1.md`.

### H. How large and diverse must the assessment bank become?

See G — and critically, growth must follow Gate 1's existing evidence-priority order (close the best-evidenced zero-content gaps first), not simply add volume to already-covered Question Types, which would repeat the exact distortion the original 18-item pool was found to have.

### I. How should Full Mock, Subject Mock and Focused Practice coexist?

As five genuinely distinct products with different purposes (workload rehearsal, subject-specific pacing, task-genre familiarity, skill-building) — not tiers or scaled versions of one another. Full detail in `CSSE_MOCK_PRODUCT_ARCHITECTURE_V1.md`.

### J. How should Continuous Writing be handled given the remaining evidence limitation?

Teaching and practice stages can be built now (task structure is well-evidenced); scoring/assessment stays evidence-gated regardless of what competitors do. Separately and urgently: the currently-live writing-feedback endpoint's own invented CSSE-attributed rubric is a pre-existing defect that should be named to the Founder explicitly, independent of this competitive exercise.

### K. What commercial expectations do these competitors create?

A free trial is likely an expected entry point (Think Academy makes it the whole page). Human review/feedback is inconsistently bundled — sometimes included, sometimes central to the product — so no single market norm exists to default to. £39.99-£69.99/month is one real anchor point for a fully-featured digital subscription tier, from one provider only.

### L. What is the smallest next implementation increment with the greatest child value?

**Unchanged from the Personalised Learning programme's own recommendation**: the single-competency, controlled-surface choice-injection pilot (`IMPLEMENTATION_DEPENDENCY_AND_GATE_REPORT.md` §4). This competitive review does not surface a reason to change that recommendation — if anything, it reinforces it: family choice is the one capability every competitor researched lacks entirely, and the one gap standing between Angel's real underlying strength and its approved differentiation.

### M. What should happen next in the current Assessment Transformation?

Continue exactly as already sequenced: the CSSE Mock structure decision stands approved; Full Mock implementation remains not-yet-authorised; the smallest next increment is the choice-injection pilot, not question-bank mass-authoring, not a commercial pricing decision, and not a Continuous Writing scoring build.

---

## Recommendation

No action beyond Founder review. This benchmark confirms rather than redirects the sequence already in motion: close the family-choice gap first (small, isolated, high-leverage), grow the question bank toward fresh-mock readiness on the existing evidence-priority order, and treat Continuous Writing's evidence gate and the live writing-feedback defect as their own, separately-tracked items — not solved by, and not blocking, anything above.

Stopping here, as instructed.
