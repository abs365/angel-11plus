## What This Tells Us, Honestly

The three providers occupy genuinely different commercial categories, not three points on one price axis:

- **Atom** sells **software access** (a subscription to a self-serve tool), with mock-test volume as the premium differentiator.
- **Essex Tutors** sells **a service event** (a physical exam sitting with human-scored feedback), priced per occurrence, not subscription.
- **Think Academy** sells **teaching time** (tutor-led classes), with mocks as a component of a broader relationship, funnelled through a free trial.

**Angel does not yet have a clear analogue to any single one of these** — it is closer to Atom's model structurally (digital, self-serve, evidence-driven) but its approved differentiation (family choice + mastery maintenance + explainability) is not a feature Atom's tiers appear to sell against at all, based on what was observed.
