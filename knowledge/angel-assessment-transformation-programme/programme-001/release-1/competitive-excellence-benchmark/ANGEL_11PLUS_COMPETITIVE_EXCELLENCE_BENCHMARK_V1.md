# Angel 11+ Competitive Excellence Benchmark V1

**Programme:** Angel Assessment Transformation Execution Programme — Release 1
**Prepared:** 2026-08-11
**Status:** Competitive intelligence only. No implementation, no pricing decision, no production change.

**Scope discipline, stated once:** the three providers below were researched directly in a real browser this session — page text, pricing pages, FAQ structure, and (for Think Academy) real parent testimonials were read firsthand, not inferred from marketing headlines alone. Coverage is bounded to the pages actually reachable in this pass (each provider's core 11+/mock-exam page, pricing/shop page, and one secondary page) — this is a real but limited sample, not an exhaustive audit of either provider's full product. Every claim below is tagged:

- **OBSERVED FACT** — directly seen on the page (a price, a feature list, a named baseline test)
- **COMPETITOR CLAIM** — the provider's own marketing statement, not independently verified (e.g. "87% received an offer")
- **INFERENCE** — a reasonable conclusion from what was observed, not itself stated
- **NOT VERIFIED** — a real, relevant question this pass could not answer (e.g. "does this provider cover CSSE specifically?")

No proprietary content (question text, papers, copyrighted material) is reproduced anywhere in this document.

## Provider 1: Atom Learning (atomlearning.com)

**Model:** digital, self-serve subscription platform, no human tutor involvement observed.

- **OBSERVED FACT** — Three subscription tiers: EMS £39.99/mo (daily Maths/English/Science practice, no mock tests), Exam Prep £59.99/mo (structured plan across all subjects incl. Verbal/Non-Verbal Reasoning, no mock tests), Exam Prep Plus £69.99/mo (adds unlimited mock tests + detailed feedback). Yearly billing saves 20%.
- **OBSERVED FACT** — Practice papers are described as downloadable/printable, marked by photographing the completed paper and uploading it for automated marking ("instant results, no marking needed").
- **OBSERVED FACT** — Claims "unlimited fresh practice papers and mock tests" as a named, marketed capability — directly relevant to Angel's own "repeated fresh mock" question.
- **OBSERVED FACT** — Named baseline tests in the site footer: 11+ baseline, CAT4 baseline, ISEB baseline, Kent baseline, West Midlands 11+ baseline. **CSSE/Essex is not named anywhere observed.**
- **NOT VERIFIED** — whether Atom's papers are genuinely tailored per named school/consortium (including CSSE) or use a generic "11+" format; the FAQ "Which schools does Atom have practice tests for?" did not render its answer text in this pass.
- **COMPETITOR CLAIM** — "87% of Atom students received an offer from their top choice school"; "standardised age score, just like a real exam." Neither independently verifiable from this pass.
- **INFERENCE** — the £69.99/mo top tier gating "unlimited mock tests" behind the highest price point suggests mock-test volume is Atom's primary premium differentiator, not adaptive teaching depth.
- **NOT VERIFIED** — Continuous Writing / extended-writing marking specifically; the page observed lists "English" as a subject but no writing-specific feature was surfaced in this pass.

## Provider 2: 11 Plus Tutors in Essex (11plustutorsinessex.co.uk)

**Model:** in-person, physical, invigilated mock exam days — not a digital learning platform.

- **OBSERVED FACT** — Real, dated, in-person mock sessions at a named physical venue (The Keene Hall, Galleywood, Chelmsford), running weekly through July-September 2026, explicitly for **both CSSE and CCHS/FSCE** (a second Essex-area consortium) — the only one of the three providers observed to name CSSE explicitly.
- **OBSERVED FACT** — Papers are described as "fully rewritten and original," written by "educational experts who use the national curriculum and student recent papers" — an original-content discipline directly comparable to Angel's own no-copying standard.
- **OBSERVED FACT** — Feedback delivered within 3 days by post: percentage score, rank within the sitting group, and named target areas. Not real-time, not digital.
- **OBSERVED FACT** — Physical exam-day requirements stated (school uniform, specific stationery) — a genuine exam-condition rehearsal, not a take-home product.
- **OBSERVED FACT** — Featured on BBC Radio 5 Live and BBC Essex per the site's own shop page (a credibility signal, not independently confirmed).
- **NOT VERIFIED** — exact pricing (the shop/product page did not render visible prices in this pass); whether any digital/online portal exists beyond booking (a single passing mention of "our online portal" was not explored).
- **INFERENCE** — this provider's core value proposition is exam-day rehearsal and ranking/benchmarking against a real local cohort, not teaching, diagnosis, or personalised remediation — it is closer to Angel's "Full CSSE Mock" concept alone than to Angel's full personalisation loop.

## Provider 3: Think Academy UK (thinkacademy.uk)

**Model:** live-tutor-led group classes (online and in-person/"offline"), global brand (part of TAL/100tal.com, a Chinese-origin EdTech company with UK, US, Singapore, Australia, Malaysia, Canada, Japan, Korea, France, Hong Kong branches).

- **OBSERVED FACT** — Primary marketed focus is **Mathematics** specifically for 11+/13+ ("We help students achieve outstanding results in 11+/13+ maths exams"), not a full multi-subject offering in the pages observed.
- **OBSERVED FACT** — Offers both online lessons and "11+ In-Person Mocks" ("offline mocks"); post-mock "analysis session" and a "review session" the following day, per real parent testimonials read directly.
- **OBSERVED FACT** — "Feedback & Progress Tracking" is a named, marketed pillar: "after each lesson parents will receive a detailed feedback & progress report."
- **OBSERVED FACT** — A parent testimonial referenced "HBS questions" appearing similar to the mock — HBS most plausibly refers to Henrietta Barnett School, a well-known London super-selective girls' grammar. **CSSE/Essex was not named anywhere observed in this pass.**
- **NOT VERIFIED** — whether Think Academy's 11+ provision covers CSSE/Essex at all, or is oriented toward London/other consortiums' exams; whether English/Continuous Writing is covered with comparable depth to Mathematics.
- **INFERENCE** — the live-tutor, cohort-class model (structured curriculum + lecture + feedback report) positions this provider closer to traditional tutoring-with-digital-support than to an adaptive, evidence-led personalisation engine.

**Headline, stated plainly:** none of the three providers observed combines everything Angel's approved direction targets — CSSE-specific evidence-led content, digital adaptive personalisation, family choice, and mastery maintenance — in one product. Each is strong in a narrower lane: Atom in self-serve volume and pricing tiers, the Essex tutor in genuine CSSE-specific exam-day authenticity and cohort benchmarking, Think Academy in live human teaching and feedback ritual. This is the opening for differentiation developed in the companion Opportunity Report — not asserted as a discovered gap without the comparison being shown first.
