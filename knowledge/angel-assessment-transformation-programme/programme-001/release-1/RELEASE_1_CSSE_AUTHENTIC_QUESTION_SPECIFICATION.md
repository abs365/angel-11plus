# Release 1 — CSSE Authentic Question Specification

**Programme:** Angel Assessment Transformation Execution Programme — Release 1
**Prepared:** 2026-08-10, per Founder direction following the migration-016 reconciliation
**Status:** Specification only. No question authored, no existing question modified, no production data touched.

**Grounding:** built primarily from the 17 Founder-Accepted CSSE Knowledge Assets (`knowledge/csse/`), via two already-approved evidence frameworks — `docs/intelligence/CSSE_ASSESSMENT_INTELLIGENCE_FRAMEWORK.md` (AEP-002, paper/section/timing/marking structure) and `docs/intelligence/CSSE_QUESTION_INTELLIGENCE_FRAMEWORK.md` (AEP-004, per-Question-Type competency/format/confidence catalogue, all 27 types, multi-year) — not repeated from scratch, per this repository's "consult, don't re-derive" convention. This document adds one layer AEP-004 deliberately stopped short of: the finer wording/reasoning-step/burden-level characteristics its own Measurement Purpose fields describe only abstractly. For that layer, this document is grounded in a fresh, direct reading of the 2022 English and Mathematics papers (CSSE-008, CSSE-011) undertaken specifically for this specification, cross-checked against AEP-004's existing 3-year citations rather than replacing them.

**Honesty boundary, stated once here rather than per-entry:** this pass adds full 20-field depth only for the 6 Question Types Gate 1 prioritises first (the HIGH/EMC-4 types with zero current Angel content — QT-RC-01, QT-RC-02, QT-MR-03, QT-MR-06, QT-MR-09, QT-MR-12). Every other Question Type retains the depth AEP-002/004 already established (competency, format, confidence, EMC, multi-year asset citations, limitations) but is not yet re-examined at wording/step/burden granularity — marked explicitly per entry as "not yet analysed to this depth" rather than silently left blank. Extending full depth to the remaining 21 types is future work, sequenced by Gate 1's own tiers, not attempted in one pass here.

---

## 1. Purpose

Define what an original Angel question must satisfy to be traceably informed by authentic CSSE examination evidence, per the Assessment Authenticity Principle now recorded in `RELEASE_1_IMPLEMENTATION_BLUEPRINT.md` §1.1. This is a specification for *what evidence a question must answer to*, not a set of questions itself.

## 2. How to Read Each Entry

Each Question Type entry below answers the 20 fields the Founder specified, grouped for readability:

- **Identity** (1-2): Official Question Type, Competency mapping
- **Structure** (3-6): task structure, skills required, reasoning process, step count/nature
- **Surface characteristics** (7-11): wording, information density, reading burden, calculation/language burden, distractors
- **Mechanics** (12-15): response format, marks, working requirements, paper position
- **Evidence quality** (16-18): year variation, progression, timing
- **Authoring guidance** (19-20): what to reproduce, what NOT to infer

### QT-RC-01 — Literal Short-Answer Retrieval

1. **Official Question Type:** Literal Short-Answer Retrieval (AEP-004)
2. **Competency:** RC-01 (Literal Retrieval from Narrative Text)
3. **Typical task structure:** A short direct question about an explicitly-stated fact in the passage, answered in the open-ruled space provided, 1 mark.
4. **Skills required:** Locate a single stated fact; reproduce it accurately without needing to interpret or infer.
5. **Reasoning process:** Retrieval only — no inference step. The answer is stated verbatim or near-verbatim somewhere in the passage.
6. **Reasoning steps:** One step (locate → write). Directly observed, CSSE-008 (2022) Q2 ("What two types of food does Priestley state was served...?"), Q3 ("What job did Inigo have to do...?").
7. **Wording characteristics:** Question stem directly names the entity/scene to locate ("What two types of food does Priestley state..."), often naming the character and moment explicitly so the candidate does not need to search the whole passage — the stem itself narrows the search space.
8. **Information density:** Low — one fact per question, no multi-clause stem.
9. **Reading burden:** Low; answer typically found within a 2-4 line span local to the question's implied moment in the passage.
10. **Calculation/language burden:** None beyond accurate transcription/paraphrase.
11. **Distractor characteristics:** N/A — open response, no distractor set.
12. **Response format:** Short open-ruled answer (1-3 lines).
13. **Marks:** 1 mark, consistently, across all cited instances (CSSE-003, CSSE-008, CSSE-013).
14. **Working requirements:** None — answer only, no working shown.
15. **Paper position:** Early in Section One, typically among the first 2-4 numbered questions (CSSE-008 Q2-4; consistent with the passage-order convention below).
16. **Observed variation across years:** None material — format, mark value, and stem style identical across all three years (AEP-004: EMC-4, HIGH confidence).
17. **Progression of challenge:** These sit at the easier end of Section One's own internal order — later questions in the same section (e.g. CSSE-008 Q7, Q15) require inference/quotation, not just retrieval, a genuine within-paper progression this specification treats as a stable structural fact (Section 6 below), not a claim about absolute difficulty.
18. **Timing implications:** Section One is budgeted 30 minutes for ~16 questions across a mixed-difficulty range; a retrieval-only item like this is one of the fastest question shapes in the section.
19. **What an original Angel question should reproduce:** A stem that names the specific scene/character and asks for one explicitly-stated fact, answerable by direct location, not inference; 1 mark; open short-answer format.
20. **What must NOT be inferred:** Do not assume a fixed word-count or line-count for the answer field — this varies by paper layout, not by evidentiary rule. Do not assume this Question Type only appears early in a section — position is a corroborating pattern (Section 6), not a defining rule.

### QT-RC-02 — Yes/No Judgement with Written Justification

1. **Official Question Type:** Yes/No Judgement with Written Justification (AEP-004)
2. **Competency:** RC-02 (Inference and Justified Interpretation); Supporting RC-01
3. **Typical task structure:** A tick-box Yes/No judgement about the passage (part a), followed by a request for 1-3 written reasons supporting that judgement (part b).
4. **Skills required:** Form a defensible judgement from the text; then produce independent, textually-grounded justification for it — tests whether the judgement can be substantiated, not just guessed.
5. **Reasoning process:** Inference-plus-justification — a two-stage demand (judge, then support), not pure retrieval.
6. **Reasoning steps:** Two linked steps: (i) form the Yes/No judgement, (ii) locate and articulate distinct supporting reasons. Directly observed, CSSE-008 Q1: "Does Priestley suggest that the weather is nice? ... Give two reasons for your answer."
7. **Wording characteristics:** Tick-box stem is short and closed ("Yes/No"); the justification stem is open ("Give two reasons for your answer") and explicitly asks for a plural, separately-bulleted answer.
8. **Information density:** Low-to-medium — the judgement itself is usually inferable from the passage's opening tone/imagery, but justification requires synthesising more than one textual signal.
9. **Reading burden:** Medium — typically requires rereading the opening lines the question points to (CSSE-008 explicitly scopes it: "Read lines 1-4").
10. **Calculation/language burden:** Written-English burden — two full, distinct reasons in "proper sentences" is an explicit mark-scheme expectation elsewhere in the same paper's rubric style.
11. **Distractor characteristics:** N/A — binary tick-box, not a multi-option distractor set.
12. **Response format:** Tick-box (1 mark) + bulleted open-response justification (2 marks, 2 reasons).
13. **Marks:** 3 total in the CSSE-008 instance (1 + 2); AEP-004 notes the reason-count varies 1-3 across years — treated as a parameter of the same format, not a different type.
14. **Working requirements:** None — direct answer only.
15. **Paper position:** Always the paper's opening question (CSSE-003 Q2, CSSE-008 Q1, CSSE-013 Q1) — a stable positional fact across all three years available.
16. **Observed variation across years:** Reason-count varies (1-3); tick-box + justification structure itself is stable 3/3 years (AEP-004: EMC-4, HIGH).
17. **Progression of challenge:** As the paper's first question, this sits at the deliberately accessible end — a judgement that's usually answerable from the passage's own opening tone, before questions requiring closer textual analysis follow.
18. **Timing implications:** Positioned to let a candidate start confidently; a 3-mark, two-part question early in a 30-minute, ~45-mark section.
19. **What an original Angel question should reproduce:** A tick-box judgement paired with a request for a specific number of independent, textually-supportable reasons written in full sentences — not a single justification, and not a judgement alone.
20. **What must NOT be inferred:** Do not assume the reason-count is fixed at 2 — evidence shows 1-3 across years; an original item should state its own required count explicitly, not default to 2 by habit.

### QT-MR-03 — Unit Conversion / Measurement Calculation

1. **Official Question Type:** Unit Conversion / Measurement Calculation (AEP-004)
2. **Competency:** MR-01 (Arithmetic Calculation); Supporting MR-04
3. **Typical task structure:** A short, self-contained conversion between stated units, sometimes standing alone, sometimes feeding into a further calculation.
4. **Skills required:** Know standard unit relationships (m→mm, etc.) and apply the correct scale factor.
5. **Reasoning process:** Direct application of a known conversion factor — not multi-step reasoning once the factor is known.
6. **Reasoning steps:** One step if the factor is known (CSSE-011 (2022) Q4a: "How many millimetres are there in 3.12m?" — one multiplication); can extend to two steps when paired with a further calculation in the same numbered question (CSSE-006 (2023) Q3 combines conversion with a further step).
7. **Wording characteristics:** Terse, single-sentence stem naming both units directly ("How many millimetres are there in 3.12m?") — no embedded narrative.
8. **Information density:** Low — one given value, one target unit.
9. **Reading burden:** Minimal.
10. **Calculation/language burden:** Low arithmetic burden alone (a single multiplication/division by a power of 10 or standard factor); burden rises only when combined with a second operation in the same question.
11. **Distractor characteristics:** N/A — open numeric answer.
12. **Response format:** Single numeric answer with stated unit.
13. **Marks:** Typically 1 mark for a standalone conversion (part of a larger multi-part question worth more overall).
14. **Working requirements:** Working space provided but not separately marked under the exact-match policy (QT-MR-14).
15. **Paper position:** Observed early-to-mid paper (CSSE-011 Q4, within the first third of the paper).
16. **Observed variation across years:** Present in all three years (AEP-004: EMC-4, HIGH); the specific unit pair varies (mm/m in 2022; distance/volume-rate conversions elsewhere) — no single unit pair recurs, only the conversion-skill genre.
17. **Progression of challenge:** A standalone-conversion instance sits near the easier end of the Mathematics paper's range; conversions embedded in a multi-step word problem (as QT-MR-03 co-occurs with QT-MR-07 in some questions, per AEP-004 §6) sit harder.
18. **Timing implications:** A fast item in isolation; slower when compounded with a further step.
19. **What an original Angel question should reproduce:** A terse, single- or two-step unit conversion using a standard UK measurement relationship, either standalone or as the first step of a compound question — not an elaborate word-problem wrapper around it.
20. **What must NOT be inferred:** Do not assume a specific unit pair recurs — no evidence supports predicting which units a future paper will use, only that some standard conversion will appear (Section 4, no AEP-003 competency names this as its own domain — flagged by AEP-004 as an open gap, carried forward here unresolved).

### QT-MR-06 — Algebraic Symbol/Unknown-Value Problem-Solving

1. **Official Question Type:** Algebraic Symbol/Unknown-Value Problem-Solving (AEP-004)
2. **Competency:** MR-02 (Algebraic/Symbolic Problem-Solving)
3. **Typical task structure:** A small system of stated relationships between letter-represented unknowns, without a sequence/machine framing, solved for one or more of the unknowns.
4. **Skills required:** Translate stated relationships into workable substitutions; solve without formal simultaneous-equations notation (Year 5/6 candidates are not assumed to know it).
5. **Reasoning process:** Substitution and deduction — express all unknowns in terms of one, then use a further given constraint to solve.
6. **Reasoning steps:** Multi-step. Directly observed, CSSE-011 (2022) Q6: "B = 2A and 2C = A. If A + B + C = 7, find (i) A, (ii) C" — requires substituting both relationships into the sum (2 substitutions), then solving the resulting single-variable equation (1 further step) — 3 reasoning steps in total.
7. **Wording characteristics:** Terse, symbolic — relationships given as bare equations, not embedded in prose narrative (contrast with QT-MR-04's word-problem framing).
8. **Information density:** Medium — multiple simultaneous facts must be held and combined correctly; a candidate must track which substitution applies where.
9. **Reading burden:** Low (short symbolic statements) but high working-memory burden — the demand is manipulative, not textual.
10. **Calculation/language burden:** Moderate arithmetic once substituted; the harder burden is correct symbolic substitution, not the arithmetic itself.
11. **Distractor characteristics:** N/A — open numeric answer per unknown.
12. **Response format:** Numeric answer(s), one per unknown requested.
13. **Marks:** Typically 2-3 marks across sub-parts (CSSE-006 (2023) Q8, Q18 similarly multi-part).
14. **Working requirements:** Working space provided; exact-match final-answer marking applies (QT-MR-14) — no credit described for correct method with a wrong final value.
15. **Paper position:** Mid-paper, observed alongside other reasoning-heavy items rather than the opening arithmetic block.
16. **Observed variation across years:** Present in all three years (AEP-004: this framework's fuller reading upgraded this from AEP-003's original EMC-2 to EMC-4, HIGH) — a genuine, disclosed upgrade, not this specification inventing new confidence.
17. **Progression of challenge:** Sits above straightforward arithmetic in the paper's own internal range — requires holding multiple facts simultaneously, a step up from QT-MR-01/03's single-fact application.
18. **Timing implications:** A slower item than direct computation; the paper's per-question time budget is not separately evidenced (no asset states per-question timing — see Section 5).
19. **What an original Angel question should reproduce:** A small system of 2-3 stated symbolic relationships (not narrative-wrapped) requiring substitution across all of them before a single-variable solve — genuinely testing symbolic tracking, not just one-step algebra.
20. **What must NOT be inferred:** Do not assume formal simultaneous-equations method is expected or taught — the papers give relationships simple enough for substitution-by-inspection at Year 5/6 level; an original item pitched at requiring textbook simultaneous-equation technique would overshoot the evidence.

### QT-MR-09 — Data Reading (Table/Chart/Graph Interpretation)

1. **Official Question Type:** Data Reading (AEP-004)
2. **Competency:** MR-01 (Arithmetic Calculation); Supporting MR-04
3. **Typical task structure:** A bar chart or table presenting a data set (e.g. daily visitor counts), followed by 2-3 sub-questions requiring a total, an average, or a costed calculation drawn from the chart.
4. **Skills required:** Accurately read values off a visual/tabular representation; then perform arithmetic on the extracted values.
5. **Reasoning process:** Extraction, then computation — a two-stage demand where the extraction step itself is a source of possible error independent of the arithmetic.
6. **Reasoning steps:** Multi-step, compounding across sub-parts. Directly observed, CSSE-011 (2022) Q15: (a) sum five days' bar values, (b) compute a 3-day mean from the same chart, (c) apply two different unit prices by day-type to the full week's data — three escalating extraction-plus-computation steps.
7. **Wording characteristics:** Short question stems referencing the chart directly ("How many visitors in total were there on the first 5 days...?") — the cognitive load sits in the chart, not the prose.
8. **Information density:** Medium-high — a 7-value data set with a secondary rule (weekday/weekend pricing) layered on top in the hardest sub-part.
9. **Reading burden:** Visual-reading burden (chart-axis reading) rather than prose-reading burden — a distinct skill from Comprehension's reading burden.
10. **Calculation/language burden:** Moderate-to-high in the final sub-part (differential pricing across 7 data points); low in the first sub-part (simple sum).
11. **Distractor characteristics:** N/A — open numeric answer; the chart itself is not a distractor set, though misreading an adjacent bar is a plausible, unstated error mode.
12. **Response format:** Numeric answer per sub-part, sometimes with a currency unit.
13. **Marks:** Distributed across sub-parts (CSSE-011 Q15 totals 5 marks across 3 sub-parts).
14. **Working requirements:** Working space provided; exact-match marking (QT-MR-14).
15. **Paper position:** Mid-to-late paper (observed as Q15 of ~21 in 2022).
16. **Observed variation across years:** Present in all three years (AEP-004: EMC-4, HIGH); the specific representation varies (table in 2023, bar chart with visitor counts in 2022, bar chart with goals in 2021) — genre stable, surface varies.
17. **Progression of challenge:** Sub-parts within one Data Reading question typically escalate (simple sum → mean → compound-rule calculation), a real observed internal-progression pattern, distinct from progression across the whole paper.
18. **Timing implications:** A multi-part question of this shape is one of the paper's more time-costly single numbered items, given three sequential extraction-plus-computation steps.
19. **What an original Angel question should reproduce:** A visual data representation (table or bar chart) feeding 2-3 escalating sub-questions — sum, then mean or lookup, then a compound rule applied across the full data set — not a single-step chart read.
20. **What must NOT be inferred:** Do not assume the chart type (bar vs. table) is fixed — both are evidenced; do not assume a fixed number of data points — 7 is observed twice but not asserted as a rule.

### QT-MR-12 — Average (Mean) Calculation

1. **Official Question Type:** Average (Mean) Calculation (AEP-004)
2. **Competency:** MR-01 (Arithmetic Calculation); Supporting MR-02
3. **Typical task structure:** Either a forward mean calculation from a given data set, or — in its harder, inverse form — reconstruction of a missing value from a stated mean and a running/updated average.
4. **Skills required:** Standard mean calculation forward; reverse algebraic reasoning about the same concept (total = mean × count) in the harder form.
5. **Reasoning process:** Forward: direct application of the mean formula. Inverse: algebraic reconstruction — treat the known mean as a constraint to recover an unknown total or new value.
6. **Reasoning steps:** Forward form: one step (sum ÷ count) or two if extracted from a chart (see QT-MR-09 overlap). Inverse form, directly observed CSSE-011 (2022) Q11: "After 7 attempts her average score is 13. (a) What is the total...? (b) On her 8th attempt she scores 21. What is her new average?" — three steps: reconstruct total from mean×count, add the new score, divide by the new count.
7. **Wording characteristics:** Short narrative frame (a named person, a game/activity) around a purely numeric operation — the narrative does not add reasoning complexity, only context.
8. **Information density:** Low-medium — 2-3 numeric facts (mean, count, new value) held simultaneously in the inverse form.
9. **Reading burden:** Low.
10. **Calculation/language burden:** Moderate — the inverse form specifically requires recognising that mean × count = total is the needed reversal, not just recalling the forward formula.
11. **Distractor characteristics:** N/A — open numeric answer.
12. **Response format:** Numeric answer per sub-part.
13. **Marks:** Typically split 2-3 across forward/inverse sub-parts.
14. **Working requirements:** Working space provided; exact-match marking (QT-MR-14).
15. **Paper position:** Mid-paper (observed as Q11 of ~21 in 2022, Q19 in 2023 alongside a weighted-mean variant).
16. **Observed variation across years:** Present in all three years (AEP-004: EMC-4, HIGH); the reverse/weighted-mean sub-format is confirmed 2022/2023 but not clearly present in the 2021 instance, which was forward-only — a genuine, disclosed asymmetry in difficulty-relevant sub-format coverage, not smoothed over.
17. **Progression of challenge:** The inverse/reconstruction form is a clear step up from the forward form — both share the same underlying competency but the reverse direction requires an extra layer of algebraic reasoning about what "mean" structurally means, not just how to compute it.
18. **Timing implications:** Forward form is fast; inverse form is one of the paper's more deliberative items despite low reading burden.
19. **What an original Angel question should reproduce:** Pair a forward mean calculation with an inverse reconstruction in the same question (as CSSE-011 does), rather than only ever testing the forward direction — the inverse form is where the genuine reasoning demand sits.
20. **What must NOT be inferred:** Do not assume the inverse form appears every year with equal weight — 2021's instance was forward-only; an original item bank should include both forms without asserting the inverse is guaranteed in any given sitting.

## 4. Remaining 21 Question Types — Inherited Depth (not re-analysed this pass)

Every other Question Type in AEP-004's catalogue (all English Comprehension types beyond RC-01/02, all Applied Reasoning, both Continuous Writing types, and the remaining 13 Mathematics types) retains exactly the depth already established there: Official Question Type name, Primary/Supporting Competency, Measurement Purpose (equivalent to fields 3-5 above at an abstract level), Confidence Rating, EMC, multi-year Asset ID citations, and Known Limitations. ## 5. What This Specification Cannot Establish (Field 20, Programme-Wide)

- **Per-question timing.** No Accepted asset states how long CSSE expects a candidate to spend on any individual numbered question — only section-level budgets (Comprehension 30 min, Applied Reasoning 10 min, Mathematics 60 min total) are evidenced. Any per-Question-Type timing figure in `QUESTION_AUTHORING_STANDARD.md` §4.5 or Angel's `estimated_time_seconds` field is an Angel-authored estimate, not a CSSE-evidenced fact — this specification does not change that status.
- **Facility rates or cohort-level empirical difficulty**, per the Founder's explicit correction to Increment 1 — no Accepted asset contains examiner statistics, pass-rate data, or per-item performance data of any kind. "Progression of challenge" fields above describe *structural* position and *compounding* of demand (more steps, more simultaneous facts), which is directly observable, not empirical difficulty, which is not.
- **AR-01 currency.** This pass's primary-source reading (2022 English + Mathematics papers) did not surface any new evidence bearing on whether Applied Reasoning remains part of the CSSE English paper post-September-2024 — the underlying question Release 0 already found unresolved (`RELEASE_0_EXECUTION_REPORT.md` Item 1). **No new evidence for Founder review to report here.** Gate 3 remains deferred, untouched by this specification.

## 6. Traceability Chain

Per the Founder's instruction, every future Angel question must be answerable through:

**Official examination evidence** (a specific Accepted Asset ID and question reference, e.g. "CSSE-011 Q6") → **Question Type** (one of the 27 in AEP-004's catalogue) → **Competency** (AEP-003's catalogue) → **Assessment characteristics** (this document's fields 3-18 for the relevant type) → **Original Angel question** (new content, structurally and demand-equivalent, never copied or superficially paraphrased) → **Educational review** (an external subject-matter reviewer, not the item's author) → **Assessment eligibility** (per `RELEASE_1_ASSESSMENT_ELIGIBILITY_MODEL.md`).

A reviewer asked "why does this question belong in an authentic Angel CSSE mock?" should be able to answer by walking this exact chain backward from the Angel question to a named Asset ID — not by pointing at a database tag.

## 7. Copyright and Originality Discipline

Per `KA-001_KNOWLEDGE_ACQUISITION_REPORT.md` §10 and the Founder's explicit instruction: no CSSE passage text, question wording, character names, specific numeric combinations, or story contexts may be reproduced or closely paraphrased in any Angel-facing question. An original Angel question must independently invent its own scenario, characters, numbers, and passage while reproducing only the *pattern* documented in Sections 3-4 above (task structure, reasoning process, step count, response format) — the same discipline `QUESTION_AUTHORING_STANDARD.md` §7 already applies to commercial 11+ publishers, extended here explicitly to the primary CSSE evidence itself, since these papers are the most directly authentic source and therefore the easiest to accidentally echo too closely.
