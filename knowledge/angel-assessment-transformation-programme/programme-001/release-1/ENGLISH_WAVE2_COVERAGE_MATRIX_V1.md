**Reconciliation note on frequency:** `wave2-fam-multiselect`'s evidence is genuinely thinner than every other family (1 of 3 years, not 2-3). This is disclosed honestly, not inflated — it's still real, evidenced CSSE structure (not invented), but Wave 2's allocation for it (6 questions) is sized to prove the family works, not to claim it's as central as quote-explain or tick-justify.

**Not pursued this wave, evidence-supported reasons:** a distinct "belief inference" sub-pattern (2021 Q12a: "does the mother believe or not believe") is close enough to `wave1-fam-tick-justify` in structure (judgement + evidence) that a separate family isn't justified without more distinguishing evidence across years. A "senses/detail-recognition" sub-pattern (2021 Q6: "which two of five senses") appears once — folded into `wave1-fam-direct-retrieval` rather than given its own family, since one year's evidence doesn't establish it as a stable, independent demand.
