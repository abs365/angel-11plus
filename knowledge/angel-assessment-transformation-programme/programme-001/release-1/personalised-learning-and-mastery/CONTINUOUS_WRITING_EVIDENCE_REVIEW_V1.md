**Programme:** Angel Assessment Transformation Execution Programme — Release 1
**Prepared:** 2026-08-11
**Status:** Evidence analysis only. No competency invented, no marking criteria invented, no writing scoring created.

**Method:** direct reading of the 4 Founder-Accepted Continuous Writing assets — no re-acquisition needed, per instruction to use existing evidence first: `CSSE-002` (undated sample mark scheme), `CSSE-004` (2023 Entry paper), `CSSE-009` (2022 Entry paper), `CSSE-014` (2021 Entry paper). Cross-checked against `docs/intelligence/CSSE_ASSESSMENT_INTELLIGENCE_FRAMEWORK.md` Observations 8-10/13 and `CSSE_QUESTION_INTELLIGENCE_FRAMEWORK.md`'s QT-WC-01a/b entries — this review both confirms those findings independently against the primary source and adds evidence neither prior document captured.

## 1. Task Structure — STABLE PATTERN (3/3 years, directly confirmed)

Every year: exactly 2 prompts, "Answer BOTH questions in your own words," each with a "you should aim to write at least six sentences" instruction. **Question 1 is always a reflective/discursive prompt answered from the candidate's own experience or opinion; Question 2 is always "Write a story based on the picture below," with a real photograph.** This is OFFICIAL EVIDENCE, independently re-verified from all 3 primary documents, matching AEP-004's existing QT-WC-01a/b finding exactly.

## 2. Timing — STABLE PATTERN

"This Section may be completed at any time during the total 60 minutes... it is suggested that candidates allow 20 minutes." Identical wording, 3/3 years. OFFICIAL EVIDENCE.

## 3. Marks — RECURRING WITH VARIATION

15 marks (2021, 2022 Entry) → 20 marks (2023 Entry). OFFICIAL EVIDENCE, matching AEP-002 Obs 9.

## 4. Content/SPAG Split — YEAR-SPECIFIC

Only the 2023 Entry paper's marker box names an explicit split: "Content Max 15 marks / SPAG Max 5 marks." The 2021 and 2022 Entry papers' marker boxes show only undifferentiated "1st marker's score" / "2nd marker's score" fields with a single total, no named sub-components. OFFICIAL EVIDENCE, matching AEP-002 Obs 13, independently reconfirmed.

## 5. New Finding, Not Previously Captured by AEP-002 — Double-Marking and Moderation Is a Stable, 3-Year Practice

**Every one of the 3 marker boxes read (2021, 2022, 2023 Entry) shows an identical structure: "1st marker's score," "2nd marker's score," and "If moderated, final score."** This is genuine double-marking with a moderation step for disagreement — a real assessment-quality practice, present in all 3 years, that neither `CSSE_ASSESSMENT_INTELLIGENCE_FRAMEWORK.md` nor any prior Angel document records. Classified **OFFICIAL EVIDENCE, STABLE PATTERN**. **ANGEL DESIGN IMPLICATION:** this is a strong signal that CSSE itself treats Continuous Writing marking as harder to standardise than the exact-match Mathematics marking or the flexible-but-single-marker Comprehension marking — Angel's own approach (currently a single LLM pass) is structurally less rigorous than CSSE's own official process, worth naming explicitly rather than glossing over.

## 6. The Only Official Qualitative Marking Guidance — CSSE-002, Undated Sample

A 4-band (Band 1-4) × 5-criterion (Ideas; Vocabulary incl. spelling; Grammar; Structure; Punctuation) rubric. Read in full, directly. Key findings:

- **OFFICIAL EVIDENCE, direct quotation of Band 4 (top band) across all 5 criteria** — e.g. Ideas: "clear and original writing... securely focussed on task... ideas explored in depth"; Structure: "securely structured... connectives used appropriately... paragraphs used accurately... varied and effective sentence structure."
- **A genuine, previously-unflagged evidence gap: the "Grammar" row is populated only for Band 4** ("secure tenses throughout"). **Bands 1, 2, and 3 have no text at all for Grammar** — the source document itself does not describe what distinguishes weaker grammar performance, only what the top band looks like. Classified **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE** for Grammar below Band 4 — this is not an Angel omission, it is a genuine gap in the only official document that exists.
- **This document is explicitly undated and generic ("Sample Mark Scheme"), not tied to any specific entry year.** It cannot be confirmed which year(s) it governed, or whether it was already in force before the 2023 Content/SPAG numeric split was introduced, or how its 5-criterion qualitative bands map onto that split. Classified **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE** — this is AEP-002 Observation 10's gap, now independently reconfirmed rather than only cited secondhand.

## 8. Sub-Competency Decomposition (§8 of the Founder's instruction) — Assessed Against Evidence

The investigative list in the governing instruction (planning, task interpretation, idea development, organisation, paragraphing, cohesion, sentence construction, sentence variety, vocabulary, grammar, punctuation, spelling, tone/register, development, conclusion, accuracy under pressure, time management, completion) was checked against the rubric's actual 5 criteria:

- **Directly named in the rubric (OFFICIAL EVIDENCE):** Ideas (partially covers task-focus/development), Vocabulary+spelling, Grammar (Band 4 only), Structure (bundles paragraphs, connectives, AND sentence variety together — not separated), Punctuation.
- **Plausible but NOT separately evidenced (EDUCATIONAL INTERPRETATION, not official):** planning, task interpretation, cohesion (implied by "connectives," not named as its own criterion), sentence construction vs. sentence variety (bundled together under "Structure," CSSE does not evidence these as distinct dimensions), tone/register, conclusion, time management, completion.
- **Not evidenced at all, in either direction:** accuracy under pressure as a distinct construct.

**Conclusion for §8: the official evidence supports a coarser 5-dimension model (Ideas / Vocabulary+Spelling / Grammar / Structure / Punctuation), not the finer 18-item decomposition the instruction offered as investigative.** Any finer decomposition Angel builds would be Angel's own **educational interpretation**, not something CSSE's evidence directly supports — must be labelled as such if used, never presented as CSSE-evidenced.

## 9a. Critical Finding — The Current Live Writing Feature Already Has an Invented, Non-CSSE-Evidenced Scoring Model

Direct read of `app/api/writing-feedback/route.ts` (the real, currently-live LLM writing-feedback endpoint): its system prompt tells GPT-4o-mini that "Essex CSSE examiners reward... Originality and imagination... Atmosphere created through technique..." and defines its own invented 0-100 scoring bands ("85-100: Exceptional — confident candidate for selective school entry"... down to "Below 40"). **None of this traces to any of the 4 Continuous Writing assets read for this review.** It does not use the real 4-band/5-criterion rubric, does not reference the real 15/20-mark totals or the Content/SPAG split, and does not reflect the real double-marking practice. This is a pre-existing, already-live example of exactly the failure mode Section 9 of the governing instruction warns against ("do not create writing scoring because an LLM can generate feedback... do not invent CSSE marking criteria") — it already happened, before this programme began. **Not fixed here** (out of this increment's stop-gate scope), but recorded as a real, load-bearing finding for the Founder Decision Report and the Excellence Model's ASSESSMENT layer, since any future Continuous Writing assessment work must treat this current endpoint as something to eventually replace, not a foundation to build on.

## 10. Answer to Gate E (Founder Decision Report will restate this)

**Evidence is sufficient to authentically model the Continuous Writing *task* — what CSSE asks, its timing, its dual-genre structure, and its formal double-marked assessment process — with high confidence (STABLE PATTERN, 3/3 years).**

**Evidence is NOT sufficient to build a fully evidence-traceable *marking/competency-decomposition* model** — the only qualitative rubric is undated, has a blank Grammar criterion for 3 of 4 bands, and never states how it maps onto the numeric mark totals. Any assessment/marking layer built now would necessarily rest on Angel's own educational interpretation for those specific gaps, not direct CSSE evidence — which is exactly the "no fabricated difficulty / no invented marking criteria" line this programme has held throughout. This finding directly shapes the Excellence Model's own internal TEACHING/PRACTICE/ASSESSMENT split (see companion document): task-level teaching and practice can be built on strong evidence; the assessment/scoring layer cannot yet claim the same evidentiary strength and must be explicitly flagged wherever it is designed.
