## 2. Candidate Question

**Passage** (original Angel content, already in use, not derived from any copyrighted or CSSE source):

> *The wind whipped across the harbour as Mira pressed herself against the cold stone wall of the lighthouse. Three weeks had passed since the keeper had vanished, and still no explanation had emerged. The light continued to sweep the dark water in its steady, mechanical arc, indifferent to the mystery it illuminated.*
>
> *She had found the notebook wedged behind a loose brick on the second landing — its pages dense with cramped handwriting, each entry growing more frantic than the last. The final entry simply read: "It knows I'm here."*
>
> *Above her, the great lens hummed and revolved. Somewhere below, the sea answered with its patient, ancient rhythm. Mira turned the notebook over in her hands. Whatever had happened here, the lighthouse held its secrets close.*

**Question:**

> Where exactly did Mira find the notebook?

**Marks:** 1

**Proposed model answer:** Wedged behind a loose brick on the second landing.

**Proposed marking guidance:** award the mark for any answer correctly identifying the location in substance (e.g. "behind a brick on the second landing" is acceptable); a vague answer that does not demonstrate genuine location (e.g. "in the lighthouse") should not receive the mark.

## 3. Intended Question Type

**QT-RC-01 — Literal Short-Answer Retrieval**, one of a 27-type catalogue Angel has derived from direct analysis of official CSSE past papers.

## 4. Intended Competency

**RC-01 — Literal Retrieval from Narrative Text.**

## 5. Intended Educational Behaviour

The learner is expected to demonstrate: reading a targeted question that names a specific object/action within the passage, locating the one sentence where that fact is explicitly stated, and reproducing it accurately — without needing to infer, interpret, or draw a conclusion beyond what the text directly says. This is the most foundational comprehension behaviour Angel's model recognises: can the child find and correctly report a stated fact, as distinct from harder behaviours (explaining what a phrase implies, comparing two characters, sequencing events) that other Question Types test separately.

## 6. Evidence Provenance

**CSSE-008** (English Paper, Main Test 1, 2022 Entry — an official CSSE past paper, freely published by CSSE for candidate familiarisation) → observed pattern: several early-section questions ask a short, character-and-scene-scoped question requesting one explicitly-stated fact, worth 1 mark each, answered in a few lines of open space, with no inference required (e.g., a question asking what two named things were served at a specific meal, or what task a named character performed at a specific moment) → catalogued by Angel's own prior evidence-analysis work as **QT-RC-01**, confirmed present with the same shape in all three years of CSSE papers Angel holds (2021, 2022, 2023) → mapped to competency **RC-01** → documented in Angel's own **Authentic Question Specification** (task structure, wording pattern, information density, reading burden, etc.) → used to author **eng-001-q5**, the item above.

No CSSE question wording, passage text, or character/scenario is reproduced anywhere in this item or this pack — only the general shape of the task (a scoped question naming a character/scene, requesting one stated fact) is described, consistent with CSSE's own copyright notice on its papers.

**The `easy` difficulty classification on this item is an educational judgement, made by applying Angel's own internal difficulty rubric (one direct reasoning step, low guessability, answer locatable in a short span) — it is NOT an empirical CSSE difficulty measurement.** No facility rate, pass-rate statistic, or any other candidate-performance data exists for this item or for the CSSE-item pattern it is based on. ## 9. Timing Disclosure

**The proposed timing estimate (45 seconds) is provisional and is not supported by any CSSE-published or empirically-measured evidence.** CSSE's own papers state only section-level time budgets (e.g. "spend about 30 minutes on this section" for the whole Comprehension section), never a per-question figure. Angel's 45-second estimate is an internal authoring estimate only, offered for your reference, not as a fact to be validated against an external source that does not exist.

## 10. Originality Assessment

This item does not copy, closely paraphrase, or lightly re-skin any CSSE question. It does not reuse any CSSE passage, character name, scenario, or specific numeric/textual combination. It reproduces only the general task *pattern* CSSE's own papers repeatedly use (a scoped question requesting one stated fact) — the same way any exam-preparation material may legitimately describe and practise a testing format without reproducing the specific questions that format has previously been used to ask.
