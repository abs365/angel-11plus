# Borrowing-Across-Zero Teaching Review

**Programme:** Angel Assessment Transformation Execution Programme — Release 1, Mathematics Reference Vertical Remediation Gate §2
**Prepared:** 2026-08-11

## What was there before

A three-bullet list: "the borrow travels all the way to the thousands column," "hundreds becomes 9, tens becomes 9, ones becomes 10," then the column subtractions. Mathematically correct, but it stated the outcome of borrowing without explaining why it happens — a learner could copy the pattern without understanding it.

## Review against the Remediation Gate's 7 questions

The worked example for 1000 − 473 (`app/learning-intelligence/learn/mathematics/arithmetic/page.tsx`, Worked Examples section) was rewritten to address each one explicitly, in age-appropriate language, with a visual place-value table (a before/after grid across Thousands/Hundreds/Tens/Ones) rather than prose alone:

| Question | Where it's now addressed |
|---|---|
| Why is borrowing required? | "look at the ones column — 0 − 3. You can't take 3 away from 0, so the ones column needs to borrow a ten from somewhere." |
| Why can't you borrow directly from a zero? | "the tens column is also a zero — there's nothing there to give. The hundreds column is a zero too." |
| Where does the value ultimately come from? | "the thousands column holds a 1, worth 1000. Regroup that 1000 as 900 + 90 + 10." |
| What happens to each affected column? | The before/after place-value table shows all four columns changing together, not just the ones column. |
| Why does the zero change? | "the single 1 from the thousands column passes down through each zero column in turn. Each one it passes through keeps a 9 and sends the rest further along, until the ones column receives a full extra ten." |
| How do the resulting digits relate to their place value? | "the 9 in the hundreds column still means 900... the 10 in the ones column means the ones place is temporarily holding ten ones." |
| How can the learner verify the subtraction? | "Check it: 527 + 473 = 1000 ✓" — an explicit, hand-verified reverse-check, not just an assertion. |

No shortcut or trick was introduced — every step remains traceable back to real place value, per the Remediation Gate's explicit "do not introduce mathematical shortcuts that hide the underlying reasoning."

## What this review is not

This is a content and completeness review against the Founder's own checklist — confirming every question the Founder listed is now genuinely addressed in the text, and that the mathematics itself is correct (527 + 473 = 1000, hand-verified). It is **not** a claim that the explanation is pedagogically effective for a real struggling child. Per the Remediation Gate's own instruction: *"Do not claim pedagogical validation after making the improvement."*
