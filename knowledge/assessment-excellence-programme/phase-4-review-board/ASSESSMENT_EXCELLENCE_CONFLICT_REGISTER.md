## AEP4-C01 — WHSG Published Admission Number, September 2026 Entry

- **Evidence Source A:** Westcliff High School for Girls' own "Determined Admissions Arrangements 2026-2027" (AEP2-026), ratified by Local Governing Board 17 March 2025 — states PAN = 192.
- **Evidence Source B:** Southend-on-Sea City Council's own "Secondary Schools Admissions 2026" booklet (AEP2-031/060/097), published later (June 2025) — states WHSG's PAN for 2026 = 184, in the same table used identically for all 12 Southend secondary schools.
- **Nature of Disagreement:** A direct, 8-place (4.3%) numeric disagreement between two Level 1 Official Primary sources about the same intake year, with no source-hierarchy tiebreaker available under this programme's evidence standard (both are official-primary, contemporaneous). WHSG's own website separately describes 192 as "a variation from the published admission number," language that is itself ambiguous about which figure is the true PAN.
- **Likely Impact:** Directly determines which of two published oversubscription-quota tables (research-notes/10: "up to 147" vs "up to 154" within-area) is actually operative for a current Year 6 family applying under this cycle. Blocks any confident statement of WHSG's 2026 intake size.
- **UPDATE (Programme 001 Release 0, 2026-08-05):** A **third independent Level 1 source** has been found: CSSE's own 2026 Entry Information Guide (AEP2-065, read in full) states WHSG's Published Admission Number for 2026 as **184** — agreeing with Southend Council (Evidence Source B) and disagreeing with WHSG's own document (Evidence Source A). This is now 2 of 3 independent official sources favouring 184. **Per `KNOWLEDGE_GOVERNANCE.md` §11.3, this does NOT resolve the conflict** — WHSG's own "Determined Admissions Arrangements" document remains a live, undated-superseded Level 1 source, and a majority count is explicitly not a valid resolution method under this programme's evidence hierarchy. Recorded as strengthened evidence, not as resolution.
- **Blocks a Decision:** **Yes** — blocks AEP4-D08 (per-school schema population) from including a single, confident WHSG 2026 PAN value.
- **Further Evidence Required:** A follow-up direct enquiry to WHSG and/or Southend-on-Sea City Council, or a later-dated official document from either source that supersedes both currently-held documents.
- **Founder Decision Status:** _[pending]_

## AEP4-C02 — SHSG Determined vs. Third-Party-Mirrored 2026/27 Arrangements

- **Evidence Source A:** SHSG's own "Determined Admission Arrangements 2026/27" (AEP2-082), hosted on shsg.org — states PAN = 224, with quotas internally consistent to that figure.
- **Evidence Source B:** A document hosted on passelevenplus.co.uk (AEP2-084, a private tutoring site), bearing genuine SHSG branding, titled "Proposed Admission Arrangements 2026/27" — states a different ratification date (24 September 2024 vs. AEP2-082's 31 January 2024) and quota figures that do not arithmetically reconcile to PAN 224.
- **Nature of Disagreement:** Evidence Level 1 (official domain, AEP2-082) vs. Evidence Level 2 (third-party host, AEP2-084) — per the evidence hierarchy, AEP2-082 is preferred and AEP2-084 is explicitly not treated as authoritative. This is procedurally resolved in favour of the official source, but the underlying discrepancy (why a convincingly-branded document with different figures exists at all) is not explained and remains unresolved.
- **Likely Impact:** Low direct impact on any Angel data decision (the hierarchy already resolves which figure to use), but a real, standing risk that a parent researching independently online could encounter and trust AEP2-084-equivalent content over the correct official figures.
- **Blocks a Decision:** No — AEP4-D08 may proceed using AEP2-082 as the operative source; the parent-facing-risk dimension is a standing caution, not a blocker.
- **Further Evidence Required:** None required to proceed on AEP2-082; understanding why the third-party mirror differs (outdated cache? genuinely superseded "Proposed" predecessor document?) would close the standing caution but is not necessary for any pending decision.
- **Founder Decision Status:** _[pending]_

- **Evidence Source B:** Phase 2's own acquired 2025 Entry and 2026 Entry Information Guides (AEP2-065/066/012/088) — official Level 1 primary sources that could confirm or refute the claim, but whose paper-structure sections were never read (only title/date were verified).
- **Nature of Disagreement:** Not a source-vs-source contradiction — a single uncorroborated tertiary claim exists, and the official documents that could settle it either way are already acquired but unread. This is an evidence gap dressed as a claim, not a genuine two-Level-1-source conflict.

Closing this requires a different evidence type entirely (an actual 2024+ English exam paper), not further reading of Information Guides. ## AEP4-C05 — CSSE Consortium Membership: 7 vs. 10 Schools

- **Evidence Source A:** This programme's own governing scope, set at Phase 2 kickoff — 7 named, fully-selective grammar schools.
- **Evidence Source B:** CSSE's own Model Publication Scheme (AEP2-070), independently re-fetched twice (once by the Phase 2 compiler, once corroborated at Phase 3/4 grounding) — states verbatim 10 current member schools, naming 3 never researched by this programme: Shoeburyness High School, St Bernard's High School, St Thomas More High School (elsewhere described as "partially selective," not full grammar schools).
- **Nature of Disagreement:** A scope-definition conflict, not a factual-data conflict — **UPDATE (Programme 001 Release 0, 2026-08-05): it turns out this DOES change year to year**, contrary to the original framing. Direct reading of AEP2-065 (2026 Entry Guide) and AEP2-066 (2025 Entry Guide) confirms: the 7-school "Grammar" scope is stable and correct (both editions agree exactly). But total Consortium membership moved from 10 (2025 Entry Guide, includes St Bernard's High School) to 9 (2026 Entry Guide, St Bernard's absent) — a real, dated membership change, not a static fact this programme mis-scoped. See `angel-assessment-transformation-programme/programme-001/release-0/RELEASE_0_EXECUTION_REPORT.md` Item 2.
- **Likely Impact:** Medium — only matters if Angel ever states or implies coverage of "the full CSSE Consortium" without qualifying which year; content specifically about the 7 named grammar schools is unaffected and now confirmed correctly scoped.
- **Blocks a Decision:** **Substantially resolved** — AEP4-D16's core scope question (which schools are full research targets) is now answered: the 7 Grammar schools, confirmed correct. Still blocks only the narrower question of whether the Founder wants the 2-3 structurally-different schools added as a future research target.
- **Further Evidence Required:** A Founder scope decision (does this programme intend to cover Shoeburyness/St Thomas More/[St Bernard's, membership-dependent on year] as a future extension?) followed by, if yes, a Phase-2-style acquisition pass for them.
- **Founder Decision Status:** _[pending]_

- **Blocks a Decision:** No — does not block AEP4-D08 (the *what* is well-evidenced even without the *why*).
- **Further Evidence Required:** A targeted search of each school's own governing-body minutes archive, or a direct enquiry, if the *rationale* for these changes is ever needed for a future Angel feature.

## AEP4-C08 — Possible 2024-2025 Cross-School "Floor-Departure" Signal

- **Evidence Source A:** SHSG's in-priority-area score moved from 303 (2021-2023, 2025) to 304 in 2024 — a single-point deviation (AEP2-089/090).
- **Evidence Source B:** WHSG (306.71 in 2025, after 4 years flat at ~303.0-303.6) and WHSB (309 in 2025, after 3 years flat at 303) each show their own first floor-departure independently, in the same 2025 admissions year (AEP2-024, AEP2-098).
- **Nature of Disagreement:** Not a factual conflict — three schools independently showing a similar-shaped, similar-timed signal, which could indicate a genuine emerging pattern or three unrelated coincidental single-year events. The evidence does not currently distinguish between the two explanations.
- **Likely Impact:** Low today (no decision currently depends on resolving this); potentially Medium in future if the pattern recurs in 2026 entry data and becomes a genuine multi-point trend worth Angel disclosing.
- **Blocks a Decision:** No.
- **Further Evidence Required:** 2026-entry actual score data (not yet available — National Offer Day for that cycle had not occurred at research date) for the same 3 schools, to see if the departure from the 303 floor persists, reverses, or was a one-off.
