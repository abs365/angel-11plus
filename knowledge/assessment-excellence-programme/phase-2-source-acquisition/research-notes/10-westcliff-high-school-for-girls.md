# Workstream 10 — Westcliff High School for Girls

**Programme:** Assessment Excellence Programme — Phase 2 (Official Source Acquisition and Validation)
**Scope:** Westcliff High School for Girls (WHSG) only. Not merged with any other CSSE Consortium school.
**Method note:** Every source below was fetched live with WebFetch and, where the source was a PDF, the raw PDF was also pulled down and read directly (WebFetch's HTML-conversion step mangles PDF binary, so the underlying PDF was read as a second, independent check). Nothing below is reported from a search-snippet alone unless explicitly labelled as such.

---

## Administering Authority

WHSG is an **academy** (Academy Converter), and its **admission authority is South East Essex Academy Trust (SEEAT)** — confirmed verbatim in the school's own 2025/26 Determined Admissions Arrangements ("The admissions authority is South East Essex Academy Trust") and in the DfE Get Information About Schools (GIAS) record (Academy Trust: South East Essex Academy Trust; Type of Establishment: Academy converter).

**Local Authority for admissions co-ordination:** Southend-on-Sea City Council (formerly "Southend-on-Sea Borough Council" — the council's own 2026 booklet uses "City Council," confirming a rebrand from Borough to City status at some point before publication). Southend-on-Sea co-ordinates the Year 7 common application process for WHSG under the School Admissions Code 2021, but does **not** set WHSG's admission criteria — the Trust does. WHSG participates in the **Consortium of Selective Schools in Essex (CSSE)**, which administers the shared 11+ selection test used by WHSG and the other Consortium grammar schools.

### Sixth Form (Year 12) Admission Number
- Consistently stated across Sources #3, #4, #5: Year 12 Admission Number is **200**, of which **184** places go to internal (WHSG Year 11) students, leaving a **minimum of 16** places for external applicants. Entry requirement: minimum 52 points (best 8 GCSEs), minimum GCSE grade 5 in English and Mathematics.

### Eligibility / Oversubscription Criteria Summary (Year 7, 11+ entry)
Consistent across Sources #3, #4, #5 (with one numerical difference noted below):
1. Testing is administered externally by the **Consortium of Selective Schools in Essex (CSSE)** — tests in English and Mathematics.
2. Registration via CSSE Supplementary Information Form (SIF); normal age eligibility is 11 on 31 August prior to admission, with a defined under/over-age exception process via a CSSE Headteacher Panel.
3. A pass mark is set annually "to ensure the entrance standard remains comparable to that applied in the period 1990 to 1994." No pupil is admitted below the pass mark, in any category.
4. Oversubscription priority order: (i) Looked-after and previously looked-after children who passed the test; (ii) children living **within** the priority-area postcodes SS0–SS9 (a defined local quota of the PAN, with a sub-quota reserved for "preferential consideration" — Free School Meals/Pupil Premium recipients — within that area); (iii) children living **outside** SS0–SS9 (small preferential-consideration sub-quota, then remaining places by rank order of marks).
5. **Numerical quota difference across document versions:**
   - 2025/26 policy (PAN 184): local (within-SS0-SS9) quota "up to 147 places" for categories 2+3 combined, plus a separate "up to 15" preferential-consideration sub-quota within that area, and "up to 4" preferential places for the outside-area category (minimum 37 places reserved for outside-area categories combined).
   - 2026/27 and 2027/28 policies (PAN 192): local quota stated as "up to 154 of the Published Admissions Number (PAN)," with "up to 15" of that reserved for preferential consideration within-area, and "up to 4" preferential places for the outside-area category.
   These figures are reported exactly as published; no attempt has been made to reconcile or explain the arithmetic — see Conflicts section.
6. Tie-break for the last place: priority to preferential-consideration children first, then straight-line distance (school's nearest pupil entrance to child's home), then computerised random allocation if still tied.
7. EHCP (Education, Health and Care Plan) children naming the school are admitted outside/before this policy, reducing the pool of PAN places available to other applicants.
8. Single-sex admissions statement: WHSG states it can only admit those who are legally female at birth into Years 7–11, per its DfE-recognised single-sex-girls-school status (stated identically in Sources #3, #4, #5).

### Waiting List Data
- WHSG's own policy documents (Sources #3, #4, #5) state: waiting lists, based on test results, are maintained per category until **31 December** of the normal admission year. Declined places are re-offered to the next-highest-scoring candidate above the pass mark in the same category (or next priority category if that category's list is exhausted). After 1 January, the process switches to the In-Year Admissions Arrangements.
- No published historical waiting-list **movement** figures (e.g. how many places were filled from the waiting list in a given year) were found for WHSG specifically, from either the school or the Council. Southend Council's booklet states general waiting-list mechanics (maintained by the Council until August each year, then by individual schools) but does not publish per-school waiting-list movement counts. **Searched, not found.**

## Possible Conflicts / Duplicate or Superseded Versions

1. **PAN for September 2026 entry is stated as two different numbers by two official sources covering the same intake year:**
   - WHSG's own "Determined Admissions Arrangements 2026-2027" PDF (Source #3), dated March 2025 / ratified 17 March 2025, states explicitly: **"For the September 2026 intake, the School's Published Admission Number (PAN) is 192."**
   - Southend-on-Sea City Council's "Secondary Schools Admissions 2026" booklet (Source #8), published June 2025, lists WHSG's **"Published Admission Numbers for 2026": 184** and its "Admission Limit 2025/26: 184" in the same table used for all 12 Southend secondary schools.
   - The WHSG Year-7 webpage (Source #1) states, for Sept 2026, "192 students," described in the fetched content as "a variation from the published admission number for this year group" — which itself implies 192 might be a number *above* a lower PAN, rather than the PAN itself, muddying which of the two figures (184 or 192) is the actual determined PAN for September 2026 entry.
   - This is recorded as an unresolved conflict between two Level-1 official sources. No attempt has been made to silently pick one over the other; both are reported as found, dated, and sourced above, per the evidence-hierarchy standard of recording rather than resolving Level-1 conflicts.

2. **Local-area quota numbers differ between the 184-PAN-era policy (2025/26) and the 192-PAN-era policies (2026/27, 2027/28):** the 2025/26 document states a combined within-area quota of "up to 147" (categories 2+3) plus a separate "up to 15" preferential sub-quota, while the 2026/27 and 2027/28 documents state a single within-area quota of "up to 154" inclusive of the "up to 15" preferential sub-quota. Reported as-published; not reconciled or interpreted by this research (out of scope — acquisition and validation only).

3. **Total-on-roll figures differ between the Council booklet and the DfE GIAS record:** Southend Council's booklet (Source #8) states WHSG's "Total on roll Jan 2025 census (incl 6th form)" as **1,342**. The DfE GIAS establishment page (Source #7) states "Current Enrolment: 1,354 pupils." These are from different data snapshots/sources (LA January census vs. DfE's own live register) and were not reconciled.

4. **Superseded/dead URLs:** the three older WHSG admissions-policy page URLs found via search (see "Categories Searched With Nothing Found," item 1) are confirmed dead (404) on the current site and should be treated as superseded/retired, not as live sources, in any future citation of this workstream.

5. **The 2026/27 and 2027/28 WHSG policy PDFs contain what appears to be an uncorrected carried-over key-dates table:** both documents list the same "2024 for 2026 arrangements" consultation and "17 March 2025" ratification dates in their front-matter table, even though the 2027/28 document is dated November 2025 and covers a different entry year. This is reported as an observation about the documents' internal consistency, not corrected or interpreted further here.

6. **Southend Council rebrand:** the Council refers to itself as "Southend-on-Sea City Council" throughout the 2026 booklet (Source #8) and on its current website, whereas WHSG's own 2025/26 policy document (Source #5) and older references use "Southend Borough Council" / "Southend Council." This reflects a real administrative rebrand (Borough → City) rather than a data conflict, but is noted for completeness since both terms appear across the source set.
