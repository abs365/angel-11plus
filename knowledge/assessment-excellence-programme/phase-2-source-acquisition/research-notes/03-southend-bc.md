# Workstream 3 — Southend-on-Sea Council Admissions Sources

Research date: 2026-08-05. All URLs below were fetched live during this research session; verification method is stated per source. Domain: southend.gov.uk (current legal name "Southend-on-Sea City Council"; historic name "Southend-on-Sea Borough Council" / "Southend on Sea Borough Council" appears in documents dated 2015–2018).

## Which of the 7 schools does Southend actually administer admissions for?

**CONFIRMED (Level 1, multiple concurring primary sources): Southend-on-Sea City Council directly administers/coordinates admissions for exactly 4 of the 7 grammar schools:**
- Southend High School for Boys (DfE number 5446)
- Southend High School for Girls (DfE number 5428)
- Westcliff High School for Boys (DfE number 5401)
- Westcliff High School for Girls (DfE number 5423)

These four appear, consistently across every version of the Southend Determined Co-ordinated Admissions Scheme read in this session (2020/21, 2025/26, 2027/28) and the Secondary Schools Admissions Booklet 2026-27, in the "List of Schools" table under Southend's own scheme, and are explicitly named in the booklet as "The four grammar schools... [that] only admit pupils through the selection test" administered by Southend's catchment (postcode SS0–SS9 priority area).

**CONFIRMED NOT administered by Southend:** Colchester County High School for Girls, Colchester Royal Grammar School, and King Edward VI Grammar School (Chelmsford — referred to in Southend's own documents as "King Edward VI Chelmsford (Boys) – school in Essex" / "King Edward, Chelmsford"). Southend's own Coordinated Admissions Scheme 2020/21 (§CSSE definition) explicitly labels these three as "school[s] in Essex," and the Secondary Schools Admissions Booklet 2026-27's "Places Offered Outside the City" table lists all three as external schools that Southend residents are occasionally offered places at, not schools Southend sets criteria for. This is consistent with the parent programme's assumption that Essex County Council administers these three (covered by a separate workstream) — Southend's own documents corroborate that division without prompting.

**Selection mechanism:** All 7 schools' 11+ test is delivered by the Consortium of Selective Schools in Essex (CSSE), a body separate from both councils. Southend is one of two coordinating local authorities feeding into CSSE-administered testing, but Southend only sets/administers the *admissions arrangements* (oversubscription criteria, PAN, waiting lists, appeals) for its own 4 schools; CSSE runs the test itself and sets the pass mark.

## Possible Conflicts / Duplicate or Superseded Versions

1. **Internal PAN inconsistency for Southend High School for Girls within the same 2026-27 booklet (Source 4).** The individual school page for "Southend High School for Girls" states "Published Admission Number (PAN): 180" in its header text, but the same document's citywide "Statistical Information about Secondary Schools in Southend" table (p.6) and the separate "Published Admission Numbers for 2026" table (p.21) both list the Admission Limit for Southend High School for Girls as **224**, and the school's own 5-year allocation table shows "Total offered on National Offer Day" jumping from 180 (2021–2024) to **224 in 2025**. This reads as a genuine PAN increase from 180 to 224 that was reflected in the summary tables but not corrected in the individual school-page header text — an internal drafting inconsistency in Southend's own official publication, not a fabrication on our part. Flagged for the register rather than silently resolved.
2. **Westcliff High School for Boys 2025 offers (191) exceed its stated PAN (185).** The booklet's own allocation table shows this as a genuine over-PAN admission in the 2025 cycle (likely appeals/EHCP-driven, consistent with the scheme's rule that "over number" admissions are managed back to PAN via attrition) rather than a data error — noted as an observation, not a conflict, since Southend's scheme documents explicitly anticipate and explain this mechanism.
3. **Legal/branding name change mid-window.** Documents dated 2015 and 2018–2019 (Sources 3 and 5) are issued under "Southend on Sea Borough Council" / "Southend-on-Sea Borough Council"; documents dated 2023 and 2025 (Sources 1, 2, 4) are issued under "Southend-on-Sea City Council." This is consistent with Southend's well-known public grant of city status, but this specific fact was not independently re-verified against an official city-status announcement in this session — noted for completeness, not asserted as newly confirmed here.
4. **Duplicate/near-duplicate "Determined Co-ordinated Admissions Scheme 2024/2025" URLs**, both 404ing — see gaps section above. If a future workstream revisit finds a live replacement URL, this should be reconciled against Sources 1–3 to fill the 2024/25 cohort gap.
5. **Southend vs. CSSE division of responsibility, restated for clarity:** Southend Council's own documents (Sources 1, 2, 3, 4) are unanimous and internally consistent that Southend sets admissions *coordination, criteria weighting (priority area/catchment), PAN, waiting lists, and appeals* for its 4 grammar schools, while the CSSE — a 10-school Essex/Southend consortium body, not part of either council — independently sets and marks the 11+ *test itself* and the pass mark. No conflict found between Southend's own documents on this division; it is stated identically across all four full-text sources read.
