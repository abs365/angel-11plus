Research conducted 2026-08-05. Scope: official primary evidence on SHSG's own admissions

## Administering Authority

SHSG is **not** administered by an LA in the ordinary community-school sense. It is a **single-academy
trust** ("Southend High School for Girls Academy Trust") — a selective, single-sex girls' grammar
school, academy converter since 1 February 2011 (confirmed via DfE Get Information About Schools,
URN 136444). The school **sets and publishes its own admission arrangements** each cycle, ratified
by its own Board of Trustees, then provided to the Local Authority.

- Home Local Authority for application purposes: **Southend-on-Sea City Council** (residents pay
  council tax to Southend City Council apply there; other-authority residents apply to their own LA).
  Confirmed directly in SHSG's own determined arrangements documents: "if you pay council tax to
  Southend City Council you apply to Southend City Council."
- The **11+ selection test itself** is administered by a third body, the **Consortium of Selective
  Schools in Essex (CSSE)**, a voluntary association of ten selective/partially-selective schools
  (formed 1993) that runs a single common test used by all ten schools including SHSG. CSSE is not
  SHSG's administering LA — it is the shared testing body referenced throughout SHSG's own policy.

### Eligibility / oversubscription criteria summary (Year 7), by cycle — all from SHSG's own documents
All three years of SHSG's own "Determined" documents (Sources 3, 4, 5) set out the same five-tier
priority order:
1. Looked-after children / previously looked-after children (LAC/PLAC) who passed the test.
2. Priority-area residents (postcodes **SS0–SS9**) qualifying for "preferential consideration" (FSM/Pupil
   Premium/LAC-PLAC-adjacent — see Preferential Consideration note in each document) — capped quota.
3. Other priority-area (SS0–SS9) residents who passed — capped quota.
4. Non-priority-area residents qualifying for preferential consideration — small capped quota.
5. Remaining non-priority-area residents who passed — fills to PAN, floor/minimum stated.

Quotas by cycle (as published on shsg.org, Sources 3 & 4; 2027/28 quotas per Source 5 are identical to 2026/27):

| Entry Year | PAN | Cat.2 (priority+pref.) | Cat.3 (priority, capped alone) | Cat.2+3 combined cap | Cat.4 (non-priority+pref.) | Cat.5 (non-priority, floor) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 | 180 | 14 | — | 144 | 3 | min. 33 |
| 2026 | 224 | 17 | 162 | 179 | 4 | min. 41 |
| 2027 | 224 | 17 | 162 | 179 | 4 | min. 41 |

Minimum qualifying/pass mark policy (stated identically in all three SHSG documents): "A pass mark
for the tests is set which ensures that the entrance standard remains comparable to that which was
applied in the period 1990 to 1994. No pupil, in any priority category, will be admitted below this
pass mark." The actual numeric pass mark is not stated in SHSG's own document — it is published
separately by CSSE (see below): **303** (standardised score), confirmed identically across all CSSE
documents found (Sources 10, 11, 12, 13).

Tie-break (identical in all three SHSG cycles): preferential-consideration status first, then straight-line
distance (LA computerised measuring system), then computerised random allocation if still tied.

## Possible Conflicts / Duplicate or Superseded Versions

1. **Two different "2026/27" documents in circulation, with materially different figures.**
   - SHSG's own site (shsg.org) hosts a document titled **"Determined Admission Arrangements
     2026/27"** (Source 4) whose key-dates table states arrangements were "Determined... ratified by
     Board of Trustees" on **31 January 2024**, and gives quotas of Cat.2=17, Cat.3=162 (Cat.2+3
     combined cap 179), Cat.4=4, Cat.5 minimum 41 — which sum consistently to PAN 224.
   - A third-party mirror (passelevenplus.co.uk, Source 6) hosts a document with the **same SHSG
     crest and title page**, but labelled **"Proposed Admission Arrangements 2026/27"**, whose
     key-dates table states "Proposed Admission Arrangements ratified by Board of Trustees" on
     **24 September 2024** (a different date), and gives quotas of Cat.2=18, Cat.3 (or Cat.2+3 combined,
     wording is ambiguous) up to 179, Cat.4=5, Cat.5 minimum 45 — figures which, on multiple
     readings, **do not arithmetically reconcile to the stated PAN of 224** the way the shsg.org version
     does.
   - This is flagged as a genuine, unresolved discrepancy between an official-domain document and a
     third-party mirror carrying the same official branding. The shsg.org "Determined" version (Source 4)
     is the more authoritative of the two (official domain, internally consistent arithmetic, and its
     "Determined" status is procedurally later than "Proposed" under the school's own key-dates
     process) but this was not independently confirmed against a School Adjudicator or LA-published
     copy in this pass. **No figure from the passelevenplus "Proposed" mirror should be treated as
     current policy** — it is recorded here only because it surfaced in search and could otherwise be
     mistaken for authoritative.

2. **Internally ambiguous wording repeated across SHSG's own documents.** Both the 2025/26 and
   2026/27 "Determined" documents use the phrase "a quota of up to [N] places is designated at this
   school for children in **both** categories 2 and 3" when describing the Category 3 cap — which reads
   as if N were the combined 2+3 total, but arithmetic cross-checking against PAN and against the more
   clearly worded 2027/28 document (which explicitly distinguishes "category 3 alone" from "overall for
   categories 2 & 3") shows N is actually the **Category 3 standalone** cap in the 2026/27 and 2027/28
   cycles. This is reported here as a source-fidelity note (the documents' own wording is ambiguous, not
   this researcher's interpretation) rather than corrected silently.

3. **PAN 2024-entry lowest score (304) is one point above the CSSE-wide minimum standardised
   score of 303**, while every other year in the 2021–2025 dataset shows the in-priority-area lowest
   score sitting exactly at the 303 floor. This is reported as a plain fact from the primary CSSE document
   (Source 12) — no explanation for the single-point deviation was sought or is available from the
   primary sources.
