# Workstream 9 — Westcliff High School for Boys (WHSB)

**Programme:** Assessment Excellence Programme — Phase 2 (Official Source Acquisition and Validation)
**Scope:** WHSB only. Not merged with Westcliff High School for Girls, Southend High School for Boys, or any other CSSE Consortium school, despite shared documents/venues.
**Task type:** Acquisition and validation only — no educational analysis or conclusions drawn.

## Administering Authority

WHSB is an **Academy Converter** (converted 1 September 2010; predecessor school URN 115317, current Academy URN 136272). Per the Southend-on-Sea City Council *Secondary Schools Admissions 2026* booklet (Introduction, p.2): *"All secondary schools in the City have academy status and therefore the academy trust is the admission authority for the school. This means that they set the schools policy (admission arrangements) and are responsible for ranking applications against their policy."*

WHSB is legally **Westcliff High School for Boys Limited**, registered in England No. 7347930, registered office Kenilworth Gardens, Westcliff-on-Sea, Essex, SS0 0BP (source: official school office email signature block quoted verbatim in a published WHSB FOI response, see below).

- **Self-administering:** WHSB (via its Academy Trust / Governing Body) sets its own admission arrangements and oversubscription criteria.
- **Coordinating authority:** Southend-on-Sea City Council coordinates the Year 7 common application process for all Southend residents (statutory duty under the School Admissions Code), but does **not** set WHSB's admissions policy.
- **Selection test operator:** The Consortium of Selective Schools in Essex (CSSE) — a 10-school consortium of which WHSB is a member — administers the 11+ selection test and publishes results/cut-off data used by WHSB.
- Essex County Council has no administering role for WHSB (Southend-on-Sea is a separate unitary/city authority); Essex CC's own admissions booklet is not the relevant LA document for WHSB.

## Key Facts Extracted (with source for each)

- **Published Admission Number (PAN):**
  - 185 for September 2023 intake, and PAN for each Year group 8–11 also 185 (Source 1, WHSB 2023 policy, §7).
  - 185 for September 2026 intake (Source 2, WHSB 2026 policy, §7; independently confirmed by Source 4, Southend LA booklet, "Published Admission Numbers for 2026" table, p.21, and again on the dedicated WHSB page, p.35).
  - 185 for September 2027 intake (Source 3, WHSB 2027 policy, §7).
  - **PAN has therefore been stable at 185 across all three officially-sourced years (2023, 2026, 2027).** No change was proposed in the 2026/27 admissions consultation (per the school's consultation notice: "No changes are proposed from the admission arrangements which are currently in place," consultation ran 25 October–6 December 2024).

- **Eligibility / oversubscription criteria summary** (materially identical across the 2023, 2026 and 2027 policy documents; Sources 1–3):
  1. Looked-after and previously looked-after children who have passed the CSSE selection tests.
  2. Children resident within the priority-area postcodes SS0–SS9, subject to passing the tests. A local quota of up to 80% of PAN (148 of 185) is reserved for this category; within that, up to 10% (15 of 148) is ring-fenced for "preferential consideration" (children receiving Free School Meals / Pupil Premium at time of test registration).
  3. Children resident outside SS0–SS9, subject to passing the tests. Up to 10% of the remaining places (4 of 37) is ring-fenced for out-of-area "preferential consideration" applicants.
  4. Tie-break for the last available place: preferential-consideration status first, then straight-line distance (LA computerised measuring system), then computerised random allocation if still tied.
  5. A pass mark is set annually so the entrance standard "remains comparable to that which was applied in the period 1990 to 1994"; no pupil is admitted below this mark in any category, and CSSE will not make offers below a total standardised score of 303 in any given year (per Source 5's standing note, consistent across all sheets 2021–2025).
  6. EHCP-named children are admitted outside this policy under separate statutory procedure, reducing the residual PAN pool accordingly.
  7. Waiting lists (main round) are held by category to 31 December of the admission year (Source 1, 2023 version, §12.a, is more detailed on multi-list membership rules than the 2026/2027 versions' §12, which is worded more briefly — see Possible Conflicts below).
  8. Sixth Form: planned intake of new external students was 30 (2023 and 2026 policy versions) rising to a stated "expected 50" alongside a "Lower Sixth" total admission number of 200 in the 2027 policy version — see Possible Conflicts below for this apparent revision.

- **Lowest offered score (published), by year** — two distinct measures found, both official but methodologically different, and neither should be conflated:

## Possible Conflicts / Duplicate or Superseded Versions

1. **whsb.co.uk vs whsb.essex.sch.uk duplication:** The URL `https://www.whsb.co.uk/attachments/download.asp?file=793&type=pdf` (found via search, hosted on the school's older/alternate domain) was fetched and its content, when read directly, proved to be the "Admission Criteria and Arrangements for Entry 2027" text — i.e. a mirror of Source 3, not a distinct document. Parents or third parties linking to `whsb.co.uk` may therefore encounter the same content under a different URL; this is recorded as a **duplicate hosting**, not a conflicting version, but flags a live risk that a future crawl of `whsb.co.uk` could return stale content if that mirror is not kept in sync with `whsb.essex.sch.uk` going forward.

2. **Sixth Form external-intake number changed between the 2026 and 2027 policy documents:** the 2023 and 2026 versions both state the "planned admission number for the intake of students (admitted to the School for the first time)" is **30**. The 2027 version restates this section with tracked-changes-style language showing a revision to "The planned Admissions number for the Lower Sixth is **200** and the intake of students… is expected to be **50**" (strikethrough of the old "30" is visible in the extracted PDF content of the 2027 document). This is a genuine, dated change between consecutive official versions — recorded here as a fact, not interpreted or evaluated.

3. **Waiting-list wording between 2023 and 2026/2027 versions:** the 2023 policy (§12.a–12.b) contains materially more detail on multi-list membership (e.g., explicit statement that a pupil cannot appear on both the "In Priority Area" and "Out of Priority Area" lists, and that lists are held "until 31 December 2023" — a year-specific date) than the 2026 and 2027 versions' shorter §12, which uses generic "31 December" wording without a year-specific anchor and drops the explicit dual-list restriction language. This may reflect deliberate simplification/redrafting rather than a substantive policy change, but the wording difference is real and is recorded rather than resolved.

4. **CSSE "Historical Guidance Sheet" file naming vs content:** the file at the URL labelled "FOR-PUBLICATION-HISTORICAL-GUIDANCE-SHEET-FOR-2026-ENTRY.pdf" in fact contains the table "Scores of last selective place offered … (1st March 2025)" — i.e., 2025-entry actual outcome data, not 2026-entry data (which would not yet exist at time of publication, since 2026 entry's National Offer Day is 2 March 2026, after this document's 13.10.2025 index date). This is normal/expected for a forward-looking guidance sheet (it uses the most recently completed cycle's data to guide the next cycle's applicants) but is recorded here so the year-label is not misread as the year the data describes.

5. **Southend LA booklet vs WHSB's own policy — total-on-roll figures:** as noted above, Source 4's "Total on roll Jan 2025 census" for WHSB (1,293, including Sixth Form) does not exactly match the DfE GIAS capacity/roll figures (Source 7, ~1,300 capacity / ~1,309 roll, no stated census date). Both are genuine Level 1 sources; the discrepancy is most likely a census-date or Sixth-Form-inclusion definitional difference, but this was not independently resolved and is recorded as an open discrepancy, not silently reconciled.

6. **2025 National-Offer-Day total (191) exceeding PAN (185):** flagged under Key Facts above; recorded as a fact present in the official LA data, not interpreted (e.g. not assumed to indicate over-admission, appeal timing effects, or data-entry variance, since the cause was not independently confirmed in this pass).
