## Administering Authority

CRGS is a **boys' academy converter** (age range 11–18), a member of **The Thinking Schools Academy Trust** (registered address Park Crescent, Chatham, Kent, ME4 6NR; Company Number 7359755). As an academy, CRGS/the Trust is its **own admission authority** in law — its "Admissions Arrangements" documents are published on CRGS's own letterhead and its own website, not issued by Essex County Council.

However, three other bodies are structurally involved and must not be conflated with CRGS itself:
- **Essex County Council (Essex CC)** — the "home Local Authority" co-ordinating body. It runs the co-ordinated secondary admissions scheme, receives the Common Application Form, sends the National Offer, and republishes each admission authority's determined arrangements (including CRGS's) on essex.gov.uk as part of its statutory admissions information duty.
- **The Consortium of Selective Schools in Essex (CSSE)** — a voluntary association (formed 1993) of ten Essex/Southend selective schools, including CRGS, which jointly organises and marks the 11+ selection test and publishes the joint "Information Guide" each year. CSSE administers testing, not admission decisions; it registers candidates and forwards standardised scores.
- Southend Borough Council — co-ordinating LA for Southend-resident applicants (not Essex-resident), named in the CSSE guide but not otherwise investigated for this workstream as it is not specific to CRGS.

Verified via: CRGS's own PDF letterhead (all three admissions-policy years fetched — see below); DfE Get Information About Schools record; csse.org.uk/schools/ member list.

## Key Facts Extracted (with source for each)

- **Published Admission Number (Year 7 / 11+):** 128, for September 2025, 2026 and 2027 entry. Source: CRGS's own Admissions Arrangements documents (Sources 1, 2, 3), each stating "The published admission number for Year 7 is 128." Independently cross-verified by CSSE's own Information Guide 2025 Entry (Source 4), which lists "Published Admission Number for 2025 — 128" for Colchester Royal Grammar School.
- **Published Admission Number (Sixth Form, Year 12, total including internal Year 11→12 transfers):** 200, stated identically in all three years' CRGS documents ("The school's PAN, including existing CRGS students transferring from Year 11, is 200.").
- **Eligibility / oversubscription criteria summary (Year 7 / 11+), as stated in CRGS's own document (identical across all three years reviewed):**
  1. Eligibility gateway: pupil must be registered with CSSE, sit the 11+ tests, and name CRGS as a preferred school on the home Local Authority Common Application Form by 31 October in the year prior to admission.
  2. **Admissions Priority 1** (up to 12 places, ring-fenced): boys scoring **greater than 320** in the CSSE standardised selection tests who are either (a) a 'looked after child' or previously looked after child, or (b) in receipt of the Pupil Premium Grant (current or within-last-6-years Free School Meals eligibility) at the time of 11+ registration. Within this priority band, looked-after/previously-looked-after children are ranked and placed first (by descending score) until 12 places are filled or the category is exhausted; only then are remaining Priority 1 places allocated to Pupil Premium-eligible applicants by descending score.
  3. **Admissions Priority 2**: all remaining places offered strictly in descending rank order of standardised score to applicants who named CRGS as a preference, until PAN (128) is reached.
  4. **Equal-ranking tie-break for the last place** (either priority category): (i) looked after/previously looked after child, then (ii) Pupil Premium-eligible child, then (iii) straight-line distance to the school as defined by the LA / set out in the "Secondary Education in Essex Booklet."
  5. Age rules: applicant must be registered in Year 6 and under 12 on 31 August immediately prior to admission; under-age/over-age applicants require Consortium Headteacher-panel approval and (for over-age) medical/exceptional-circumstance evidence; the test can only be sat once.
  6. **CSSE-wide floor** (separate document, Source 4): no CSSE school may offer a place to any child scoring below a total standardised score of 303, appeals/special-consideration cases excepted. This is a consortium-wide minimum, distinct from CRGS's own internal 320 Priority-1 threshold.
  7. Years 8–10 occasional-vacancy admissions: school-set tests in English, Maths, Science; tie-break is looked-after-child status then distance.
  8. Sixth Form: minimum 38 points across best 5 GCSEs (with a stated points-conversion table), grade 7+ in A-level subjects, grade 5+ in GCSE English/Maths; priority to internal Year 11 transfers; external applicants ranked by average GCSE points score (grade-9 count as secondary tie-break), then looked-after-child status, then distance.
- **Lowest offered score (if published), by year:** **Not verified as an official primary source in this research pass.** CRGS's and CSSE's own published documents explicitly state that actual cut-off/"last child admitted" scores are only known after National Offer Day each March and are not published as fixed figures in the policy documents themselves (CSSE Source 4: "The actual cut off scores are only known after national offer day in march" and a separate historical "guidance sheet" is referenced as being published on the CSSE website each October — this specific guidance sheet was not located/fetched in this pass). A general web search surfaced two Freedom-of-Information request pages on WhatDoTheyKnow.com apparently addressed to CRGS and to CSSE, with titles referencing cut-off marks for 2017–2021 entry and "scores of last selective place offered... National Offer Day 3rd March 2025" — but **WhatDoTheyKnow.com returned HTTP 403 to every fetch attempt**, so none of this could be independently read or verified, and per the task rules no score figures from these unverified pages are reported here. This is recorded as a genuine gap, not filled by inference.
- **Waiting list data (if published):** CRGS's own document (all three years) states only that "The school will hold a waiting list for the Autumn Term following the September entry," and that parents wishing their child to remain on the list beyond that term must contact the school directly. **No published waiting-list movement figures, list length, or offers-made-from-waiting-list data were found** in any official source checked.
