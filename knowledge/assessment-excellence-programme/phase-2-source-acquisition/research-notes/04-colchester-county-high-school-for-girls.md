## Administering Authority

CCHSG is a **Grammar Academy (Girls)**, part of **ALPHA TRUST** multi-academy trust. As an academy, the school's **governing body (Local Governing Body) is its own admissions authority** — it sets and determines its own admissions arrangements — but **Essex County Council (ECC)** acts as the **coordinating local authority**: it receives applications via the Common Application Form, holds waiting lists, publishes the countywide "Schools Admission Policies Directory" and "Secondary Education in Essex" booklet, runs the offer process (National Offer Day), and manages appeals administration (Clerk to the Independent Appeals Panel). Test administration is delegated by the school to the **Consortium of Selective Schools in Essex (CSSE)**, which acts "as agent for the School" for the 11+ selection test used by all CSSE member grammar schools.

Source: CCHSG's own A1 Admissions Policy documents (all years, §1.2 "Admission arrangements"); Essex County Council "Schools Admission Policies Directory 2026/2027" (essex.gov.uk).

## Key Facts Extracted (with source for each)

**Published Admission Number (PAN):**
- September 2024 entry: 192 (Source 4, §1.1)
- September 2025 entry: 192 (Source 3, §1.1)
- September 2026 entry: 192 (Sources 1, 2, 6, 8)
- September 2027 entry: 192 (Source 5, §1.1)
- PAN was increased by 32 places to 192, effective September 2020, from an implied prior PAN of 160 (Source 9, FAPP Summary). No official document was found stating the PAN for entry years earlier than September 2020.
- Sixth Form (Year 12) intake: up to 50 external students per year in 2024-25, 2025-26 and 2026-27 policies (Sources 4, 3, 1/2, §2.1); the 2027-28 policy uses identical wording but changes eligibility to "50 boys and girls" (Source 5, §2.1).

**Eligibility / oversubscription criteria summary (as currently stated for September 2026 entry — Sources 1, 2, 6, 8):**
1. Children with an EHCP naming CCHSG who score 320+ are admitted first (reduces the effective PAN).
2. Looked After Children (LAC) / Previously Looked After Children (PLAC) scoring 320+.
3. Up to 20 places to the highest-scoring Pupil-Premium-eligible applicants scoring 320+.
4. Up to 10 places to the highest-scoring Service-Pupil-Premium-eligible applicants scoring 320+ AND living within the 25-mile Priority Area.
5. Remaining places up to 154 (cumulative, including 1-4) allocated by descending score to applicants living within the 25-mile Priority Area.
6. Remaining 38 places allocated by descending score regardless of residence.
7. Tie-break: Pupil-Premium eligibility first, then straight-line distance to school.
- **This is a materially different structure from the 2024-2025 policy (Source 4)**, which had no Priority Area and no SPP category at all — see "Possible Conflicts" below.
- Test: two papers, English (50%) and Mathematics (50%), administered/marked under CSSE coordination; standardised and aggregated into a single score (all years, consistent).

## Possible Conflicts / Duplicate or Superseded Versions

1. **Real, documented change in admissions arrangements between 2024-25 and 2025-26 policies (not a data-quality conflict — a genuine policy change):** The 2024-2025 policy (Source 4) has no 25-mile Priority Area and no Service Pupil Premium (SPP) reserved-places category. The 2025-2026 policy (Source 3) onward introduces both: a 25-mile Priority Area, up to 10 places reserved for SPP-eligible applicants within that area, and a restructured cascade (EHCP → LAC/PLAC → Pupil Premium → SPP-within-area → within-area rank order → outside-area rank order). This is confirmed by directly reading both policy documents in full. No public consultation document evidencing this change was located (see "Categories Searched" above) — flagged for founder awareness as a Level 1 fact (the change itself is verified) with a gap in the supporting-consultation evidence trail.

2. **Internal inconsistency in September 2025 within-area cut-off score, across two official CCHSG documents:** Source 7 (National Offer Day PDF) states 321-322; Source 8 (FAQ PDF) states 323-324. Both are CCHSG's own current publications. Not reconciled.

3. **Internal inconsistency in September 2025 entry applicant/tested-candidate counts, across two official CCHSG documents:** Source 1/2 (2026-27 policy) states 630 tested / 697 applicants for Sept 2025 entry; Source 5 (2027-28 policy) states 626 tested / 726 applicants for the same cycle. Both figures purport to describe the identical historical fact. Not reconciled.

4. **Essex.gov.uk-hosted PDF filename vs. content mismatch:** The URL filename "2025 Colchester County High School for Girls (5454)_0.pdf" and its August-2025 upload-path both suggest a "2025" document, but the content is explicitly the **2026-2027** cycle policy. Similarly, "Colchester-County-High-School-For-Girls-5454-2023.pdf" contains the **2024-2025** cycle policy. Essex CC's filenames/upload dates track when the file was uploaded, not the entry-year the policy covers — anyone citing these URLs by filename alone should verify the actual entry year stated inside the document.

5. **Two distinct identifier schemes in play, easily confused:** DfE URN = **137515** (used by GIAS, and confirmed independently on the FAPP Summary document). Essex CC's own internal "School Number" = **881-5454** (used throughout the Essex Admissions Directory). These are not the same number and must not be conflated when cross-referencing DfE vs. LA sources.

6. **Unverified second GIAS URN (115370):** surfaced in a search snippet for "Colchester County High School for Girls" but not independently confirmed or used as a source for any fact above. Possible closed/predecessor establishment record; flagged, not resolved.

7. **GIAS-listed capacity/roll figures vs. Essex Directory figures:** GIAS's auto-summarised page stated capacity "1,020 pupil places" with enrolment "1,198," which is higher than and inconsistent with the Essex Directory's "Number on roll Jan 2025: 959." Not reconciled — GIAS's summary figures were not independently drilled into further (would require fetching GIAS's underlying census data pages, which was outside this workstream's time budget).
