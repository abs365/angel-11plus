# Workstream 7 — Southend High School for Boys (SHSB)

Scope note: this record covers SHSB's own admissions arrangements only. It is not merged
with Southend High School for Girls, Westcliff High School for Boys/Girls, or any other
CSSE Consortium school.

## Administering Authority

SHSB is its own admission authority. It is an academy converter (single-academy trust),
URN 136443, DfE number 5446. As stated in the school's own "Admission criteria and
arrangements" documents: "The school determines admission in the order of priority set out
below" (para 10) — i.e. the Governing Body, not the Local Authority, sets and applies the
oversubscription criteria.

However, **applications are processed through Southend-on-Sea City Council's coordinated
admissions scheme** (Southend-on-Sea is the relevant Local Authority — SHSB is NOT in
Essex County Council's administrative area, despite testing being run by the Essex-branded
CSSE consortium). The council collates preferences, ranks non-selective criteria elements
(e.g. distance), and issues the National Offer Day letters; the school itself ranks applicants
against its own published criteria and returns ranked lists to the council.

The **11+ test itself** (English and maths) is administered by the Consortium of Selective
Schools in Essex (CSSE), a ten-school consortium covering Essex and Southend grammar
schools. CSSE state on their own site: "The CSSE Office deals only with examination
administration. It has no involvement in school applications, admissions or appeals." CSSE
membership confirmed: King Edward VI GS, Colchester County High School for Girls,
Colchester Royal Grammar School, Southend High School for Girls, Southend High School
for Boys, Westcliff High School for Girls, Westcliff High School for Boys, St Bernard's High
School, St Thomas More High School, Shoeburyness High School.

## Key Facts Extracted (with source for each)

- **Published Admission Number (PAN):**
  - Entry 2025: PAN = 180 (Source 2, para 7: "For the September 2025 intake, the school's published admission number (PAN) is 180. The PAN for each year group (8-11) for 2025 is 180.")
  - Entry 2026: PAN = 180 (Source 1, para 7, identical wording for 2026)
  - Entry 2027: PAN = 180 (Source 3, para 7, identical wording for 2027)
  - No published-admission-number change was found across 2025–2027. Figures before 2025 were not located (see gaps below).

- **Eligibility / oversubscription criteria summary** (consistent across the 2025, 2026 and 2027 SHSB policy documents — Sources 1–3, paras 1–12):
  1. Eligibility to sit the test: children who will be aged 11 on 31 August immediately prior to admission; under/over-age applicants require CSSE Headteacher-panel approval (Note E in the documents).
  2. Test: English and maths, administered by CSSE at a CSSE-determined venue/date; SHSB itself is not the assessor.
  3. A pass mark is set annually by CSSE "to ensure the entrance standard remains comparable to that applied 1990–1994"; no pupil in any category is admitted below this mark.
  4. Priority order for admission: (i) Looked-after/previously looked-after children who passed; (ii) children resident **within** the priority-area postcodes SS0–SS9 — quota of up to 5/6ths of PAN (150 of 180), of which 10% (15 places) is reserved for "preferential consideration" (Free School Meals / Pupil Premium recipients) applicants; (iii) children resident **outside** SS0–SS9 postcodes — remaining ~1/6th of PAN (30 places), of which 10% (3 places) is reserved for preferential-consideration applicants.
  5. Within each category, places go by rank order of test mark, highest first.
  6. Tie-break for the last place: preferential-consideration status first, then straight-line distance from home to nearest school pupil entrance (LA's computerised measuring system), then random computerised allocation if still tied.
  7. EHCP naming the school: admitted ahead of other applicants (statutory), reducing places available within the PAN.
  8. Statement on single-sex admissions: school states it follows the School Admissions Code 2021 and, per its own published position, does not admit transgender applicants into Years 7–11 on the basis of biological-sex definitions (this is the school's own stated legal interpretation, recorded here as their published position, not independently adjudicated).

- **Lowest offered score (if published), by year:**
  - **2023 entry only:** Inside Priority Area = 303 (the CSSE-wide minimum standardised score floor); Outside Priority Area = 342. Source: CSSE "Scores of last selective place offered … National Offer Day 1st March 2023" (Source 6).
  - No equivalent official score-cutoff table was found for any other year (2016–2022, 2024, 2025, 2026, or 2027 entry). Third-party 11+ tutoring sites (e.g. passelevenplus.co.uk) claim additional years' figures (e.g. 2022 entry: 303 inside / 337 outside) but these were NOT independently verified against an official CSSE or SHSB primary source in this pass and are therefore NOT reported as verified facts.

- **Waiting list data (if published):**
  - No published waiting-list *numbers* (i.e., how many names, how far lists moved) were found for any year.
  - The *rules* governing waiting lists are published (Sources 1–3, para 12): lists are maintained by category until 31 December; a declined place triggers an offer to the next-highest-scoring candidate in the same category (or next priority category if that list is empty); after 1 January, waiting lists convert to the in-year admissions process, which may include re-testing.
