# Workstream 6 — King Edward VI Grammar School, Chelmsford (KEGS)

Phase 2, "Official Source Acquisition and Validation," Angel 11+ Assessment Excellence Programme.
Scope: KEGS only. No merging with other CSSE consortium schools' records.

## Administering Authority

KEGS is **its own admissions authority**. It is an academy converter (single-academy trust; company limited by guarantee, registered in England and Wales, company number 07563345). Each admissions policy PDF is headed "KING EDWARD VI GRAMMAR SCHOOL" and states "APPROVED BY TRUSTEES [month/year]" — confirming the school's own Trustees, not Essex County Council, set the policy.

Essex County Council (Essex CC) plays a **coordinating/secondary role**: it operates the online Common Application Form and coordinated admissions scheme, hosts mirrored copies of the school's admissions policy PDFs on essex.gov.uk, runs the straight-line-distance calculation service, publishes mid-year application forms, and administers the **Statutory Appeals Panel** referenced in every version of the policy ("Clerk to the Independent Appeal Panel, P.O. Box 11, Chelmsford, Essex, CM1 1LX").

The **CSSE (Consortium of Selective Schools in Essex)** — a separate body of which KEGS is a member alongside Colchester Royal Grammar School, Southend High School for Boys/Girls, Westcliff High School for Boys/Girls, and Colchester County High School for Girls — administers the shared 11+ selection test itself (registration, test day, marking logistics) on behalf of member schools, but KEGS's own staff mark the papers and set KEGS's own oversubscription criteria.

## Key Facts Extracted (with source for each)

- **Published Admission Number (current, 2025/2026/2027 entry):** 150 boys into Year 7 (of which up to 120 are Priority-Area-linked and up to 30 are open). — Sources 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 (all state "The number intended to admit to King Edward VI Grammar School (KEGS) is 150," identically worded across all three policy years read in full)

- **Historical PAN (2011/12, pre-priority-area, for 2013/14 entry):** 112 total places, of which the consultation proposed reserving a minimum of 90 (80%) for in-area candidates. — Source 8 (2011 consultation letter, read in full)

- **PAN increase from 120 to 150 effective September 2015:** This specific figure ("increased from 120 to 150 places, effective September 2015 intake") appeared repeatedly in AI-generated web-search summaries drawing on kegs.org.uk and third-party sites (e.g. Wikipedia), but **could not be independently verified against a verbatim quotation from an official KEGS or Essex CC primary document** in this research pass. It is reported here as an **unverified claim requiring further primary-source confirmation**, not as a confirmed fact. This leaves a gap between the confirmed 2011 figure (112 places) and the confirmed current figure (150 places): the exact year(s) and size of the intervening increase(s) are not independently evidenced.

- **Eligibility / oversubscription criteria summary (current policy, all years read in full — 2025/2026/2027 entry, structure identical, thresholds vary by year):**
  - One shared CSSE 11+ selection test (English + Mathematics), marked by KEGS's own staff; test open to Essex residents and applicants from outside the county; boys only for Year 7–11 entry (Sixth Form is mixed-gender, GCSE-results-based, no test).
  - Places offered first to EHCP-named applicants, then in rank order of standardised (age-adjusted) score.
  - 120 of 150 places reserved for boys **continuously resident within the 12.5-mile Priority Area** (measured by straight-line distance, Post Office/Ordnance Survey data, via Essex CC) since a fixed date in Year 6 — **31st October in Year 6** for 2025 and 2026 entry; changed to **10th October in Year 6** for 2027 entry (a genuine, source-confirmed policy change between years).
  - Within the 120 priority-area places, up to 8 are reserved for Pupil-Premium/looked-after/previously-looked-after/internationally-adopted-previously-looked-after applicants who reach a lower qualifying standardised score: **330** for 2026 and 2027 entry, **340** for 2025 entry (source-confirmed year-on-year change).
  - Remaining 30 places open to all, regardless of residence, ranked purely by score; within these, up to 2 reserved for the same disadvantaged-pupil categories at a standardised score of **350** (consistent across 2025/2026/2027 entry).
  - Tie-break order for the last place: looked-after/previously-looked-after/internationally-adopted-previously-looked-after children → Pupil Premium recipients → closest straight-line distance → drawing of lots by an independent person.
  - Casual entry (Years 8–11): school's own internal tests, not equivalent to the 11+; rank-ordered shortlist.
  - Sixth Form: GCSE-results-based (minimum average points score 6.625 across best 8 subjects; Grade 6+ required in GCSE Maths and English); no Priority Area test applies; minimum age criterion differs across years read ("under 17 on 31 August" in the 2025 and 2026 policies vs "16 years of age... on 31st August" in the 2027 policy — a genuine wording/substance difference across years, flagged as-is, not resolved by this research).
  - 2025-entry policy uniquely (of the three full years read) also required Sixth Form applicants to hold **GCSE grade 4+ in a modern foreign language (or Latin)** — this specific requirement is **absent** from the 2026 and 2027 entry policies' Sixth Form sections as read in full. Genuine textual difference across years, flagged for the record.
  — Sources 1, 3, 4 (full text read for all three years)

- **Lowest offered score (published), by year:**
  - **September 2025 entry:** in-area lowest offered score **354**; out-of-area lowest offered score **367** (both "rounded to the nearest whole number," per the school's own verbatim wording). — Source 6, verbatim quotation captured via WebFetch: "For the students starting Year 7 at KEGS in September 2025, the lowest score obtained by an 'in area' applicant, who was neither in receipt of the Pupil Premium payment nor classified as a looked after child, was 354... the lowest score obtained by an 'out of area' applicant... was 367."
  - **No lowest-score data found published, in a source this research could independently verify by exact quotation, for any other year** (2016–2024, 2026). Third-party tutoring/11+-advice sites (e.g. findmyschool.uk) circulated figures for a "Priority Score (224)" and specific distances (5856m for 2023 entry, 5625m for 2024 entry, 5028m for 2022 entry, "0 miles" for 2024 entry) attributed to Essex CC secondary-transfer outcome reports, but **this research could not locate or independently verify the underlying essex.gov.uk report itself** — these figures are recorded here as **unverified third-party claims, not confirmed**, and should not be treated as Level 1 evidence.

- **Waiting list data (published):**
  - Policy text (all years read in full) confirms: a waiting list of remaining test-takers is held by the school for **one year following admission to Year 7**; drop-out places are re-offered strictly in rank order within the same category (priority-area vs open) the departing pupil held. — Sources 1, 3, 4
  - FAQ page (Source 6) gives one year's near-miss numbers, verbatim: "Out of the students applying for a place in September 2025, there were approximately 34 students living 'in area' who applied to KEGS, were not offered a place and who achieved a score that was within 5 marks of the lowest score achieved by a successful 'in area' candidate. Similarly, there were approximately 28 students living 'out of area'... within 5 marks of the lowest score achieved by a successful 'out of area' candidate." No further waiting-list movement/offer data (e.g. how many of these were subsequently offered places, or by what date) was found published for any year.

## Possible Conflicts / Duplicate or Superseded Versions

1. **PAN history gap:** Confirmed 112 places (2011, pre-priority-area) vs confirmed 150 places (2025/2026/2027, current) — the claimed intermediate step ("120 → 150 from September 2015") is repeated across multiple secondary/tutoring sites but was **not verified against a primary document** in this pass. Flagged as an open gap, not resolved.

2. **Musical-aptitude admission route (2011 consultation proposal):** The 2011 letter proposes reserving up to 10% of places for a "significant musical aptitude" pathway. This route **does not appear anywhere in the current (2025/2026/2027) admissions policies read in full** — there is no musical-aptitude criterion in the current oversubscription structure. Either the 2011 proposal was not adopted, or it was adopted and later withdrawn; this research did not locate a document confirming either outcome. Flagged as an unresolved historical thread, not a live conflict in current policy.

3. **Qualifying-score thresholds change year to year** (330 vs 340 for the priority-area disadvantaged-pupil route between 2025 and 2026/2027 entry) — confirmed genuine, not a duplication error; each year's PDF is internally consistent and dated/Trustee-approved separately.

4. **Priority-area residency reference date changed** from "31st October in Year 6" (2025, 2026 entry policies) to "10th October in Year 6" (2027 entry policy) — confirmed genuine textual change across the two most recent policy versions, not a transcription artefact (verified by reading both PDFs in full independently).

5. **Sixth Form modern-foreign-language GCSE requirement** present in the 2025-entry policy's Sixth Form admission criteria but absent from the 2026- and 2027-entry policies' equivalent sections — flagged as a genuine textual difference across the three full documents read; not resolved or explained by any source found in this pass.

6. **Sixth Form minimum-age wording** differs between "under 17 years of age on 31st August" (2025, 2026 entry policies) and "16 years of age on 31st August" (2027 entry policy) — flagged as a genuine textual difference, not resolved.

7. **Third-party "lowest score/distance" figures** (findmyschool.uk-attributed: Priority Score 224, distances 5028m/5856m/5625m for 2022/2023/2024 entry) directly **conflict in form** with the school's own FAQ page figures for 2025 entry (scores 354/367, not expressed as a "Priority Score" or in metres) — the two data sets appear to use different measurement conventions (one in raw/standardised score terms, one apparently referencing an older "Priority Score" methodology) and were **not reconcilable** with the sources available in this pass. Neither the third-party figures nor their claimed methodology could be traced to a verifiable official Essex CC document. Treat the third-party figures as **unverified** pending a located primary source.

8. **Two schools share the "King Edward VI Grammar School" name in GIAS** (URN 136642 Chelmsford vs URN 142262 Louth, Lincolnshire) — confirmed distinct and unrelated; flagged explicitly so this workstream's evidence is never merged with a Louth-sourced document by a future researcher.
