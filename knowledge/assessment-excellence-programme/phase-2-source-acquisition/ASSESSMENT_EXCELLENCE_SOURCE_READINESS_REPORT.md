# Assessment Excellence Source Readiness Report

**Programme:** Angel 11+ Assessment Excellence Programme — Phase 2 (Official Source Acquisition and Validation)
**Status:** Phase 2 acquisition/validation work complete. This report is submitted for **Founder review and approval** before Phase 3 (educational analysis) may open, per the programme's hard gate.
**Prepared:** 2026-08-05
**Scope discipline maintained throughout:** no educational analysis, no gap analysis of Angel's own content, no assessment redesign. This report evaluates evidence readiness only.

---

## How this acquisition was run

10 independent research workstreams were dispatched in parallel — one for the CSSE Consortium itself, one each for Essex County Council and Southend-on-Sea City Council, and one for each of the 7 named schools individually (never merged). Each workstream searched for, fetched, and verified official sources against the Level 1 / Level 2 / Level 3 evidence hierarchy in the governing instruction, explicitly disclosing what it searched for and could not find rather than omitting gaps silently.

Every workstream's raw findings were then independently compiled by a second pass (not simply copied from the research agent's own summary): claimed URLs and figures were checked against the workstream's own stated verification method, and 4 load-bearing sources were independently re-fetched from scratch by the compiler as spot-checks (a CSSE consortium-wide score table, two school admissions pages, one CSSE index page) — all 4 matched the workstream reports exactly. Progress was checkpointed to disk after every workstream, per standing checkpoint discipline, so no work existed only in memory at any point.

**Result: 101 sources acquired and catalogued** (`AEP2-001` through `AEP2-101`) in the permanent `ASSESSMENT_EXCELLENCE_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md`, indexed in `ASSESSMENT_EXCELLENCE_SOURCE_REGISTER.md`. Full per-workstream detail (search process, verbatim quotations, every category searched with nothing found) is preserved in `research-notes/01-*.md` through `research-notes/10-*.md` and is not repeated in full here.

- **Content of 7 CSSE-published PDFs** that are confirmed live, correctly-typed, and on the official domain, but are image-only scans this environment could not render or text-extract (4 Standardisation Reports, the candidate-volume report, the Photographing policy, the Atom Learning PDF).

## 5. Coverage by Admissions Evidence

**Strong:** current oversubscription criteria, tie-break rules, and Priority Area definitions are fully documented for all 7 schools, cross-verified in most cases against an independent Local Authority-hosted copy of the same document.

**Weak:** no consultation document was found for any of the 3 confirmed real policy changes identified in this acquisition (CCHSG, SHSG, KEGS). Under the DfE School Admissions Code, admission authorities are required to publicly consult before determining materially changed arrangements — the absence of these documents is either a genuine publication gap by the schools/LAs, or the documents exist but were not discoverable via the search methods available in this environment (in particular, Wayback Machine / web.archive.org was blocked in every workstream that attempted it, and none succeeded in reaching it as an alternative route).

**Absent:** no waiting-list movement statistics exist anywhere in the acquired corpus, for any school, any year.

## 6. Coverage by Standardisation Evidence

**Strong.** CSSE's own age-standardisation methodology statement was acquired and read in full. Of 7 years of Standardisation Reports (2019-2025 Entry) found on CSSE's official domain, 3 were independently content-verified by direct text extraction; the other 4 are confirmed to exist, resolve, and be correctly-typed PDFs on the official domain, but their internal content could not be read in this environment (image-only PDF format). The CSSE-wide minimum standardised-score floor of 303 is independently confirmed, identically, across at least 6 separate CSSE documents spanning different workstreams and years — this is the single most consistently corroborated fact in the entire acquisition.

## 7. Coverage by Admissions Evidence (Local Authority statistical publications)

Southend-on-Sea City Council publishes the richer statistical picture of the two councils: a single 47-page booklet gives 5 years (2021-2025) of PAN, offers, and appeals data for all 4 of its grammar schools in one place. Essex County Council's equivalent Directory gives current-year PAN for its 3 schools but — notably — omits the "applications received" statistic specifically for its 3 selective schools while providing it for ordinary comprehensives, and provides no multi-year back-series at all for CRGS, CCHSG, or KEGS. **This is a genuine, structural asymmetry in what the two administering authorities publish, not a research gap on this programme's part** — it means Southend-administered schools (SHSB, SHSG, WHSB, WHSG) currently have a materially richer statistical evidence base than Essex-administered schools (CRGS, CCHSG, KEGS) for any future longitudinal work.

## 9. Known Limitations

1. **Wayback Machine / web.archive.org was unreachable from this environment in every workstream that attempted it.** This is very likely the single largest recoverable source of the pre-2023 policy-text gap — several workstreams found search-engine evidence that older policy pages once existed at now-404 URLs, which is exactly the kind of content web.archive.org typically preserves. This is an environment limitation, not a "does not exist" finding, and is the top recommended follow-up action.
2. **No PDF-to-image rendering tool was available in this environment**, leaving 7 official CSSE PDFs (and none from any council or school) confirmed-to-exist but content-unread.
3. **No source in this acquisition has yet been physically mirrored into this repository's own storage** — every entry's "Local Repository Location" is a live external URL (with one exception, the CSSE score table mirrored during compiler spot-checking). This acquisition pass prioritised breadth and verification over local archiving; mirroring the highest-value sources (the CSSE Historical Guidance Sheets, Standardisation Reports, and each school's current policy) is a straightforward, low-risk follow-up action, not a re-acquisition.
4. **CSSE's own Publication Scheme states it has 10 member schools, not the 7 this programme scopes** (adding Shoeburyness High School, St Bernard's High School, St Thomas More High School — all described elsewhere in the acquired evidence as "partially selective," distinct from the 7 fully-selective grammar schools named in the governing instruction). This is recorded as a scope-accuracy observation for the Founder's decision, not resolved by this compilation.
5. **This programme's Phase 1 Blueprint** (referenced as "complete" in the Founder's Phase 2 instruction) does not currently exist as a file anywhere in this repository — it appears to have existed only in a prior conversation's context. This does not block Phase 2's own completion (which is source acquisition, not blueprint-dependent), but is flagged so it is not assumed to exist by a future phase.

## 10. Risks to Phase 3

1. **Trend-analysis risk.** The 2016-2022 evidence base is too thin, for most schools, to support any claim about how the examination or admissions landscape changed over time in that period. Phase 3 must not present the well-evidenced 2023-2027 picture as though it also characterises 2016-2022 — that would be exactly the "complete but invented" failure mode the governing instruction prohibits.
2. **Conflict-propagation risk.** The 2 unresolved Level-1 conflicts (WHSG PAN, SHSG determined-vs-mirror) must be carried into Phase 3 as explicit open questions, not silently resolved by picking whichever figure is more convenient.
3. **Scope-definition risk.** Phase 3 must decide, with Founder input, whether "the CSSE Consortium" for this programme's purposes means the 7 grammar schools named in the governing instruction or the 10 schools CSSE itself claims as members — this affects any consortium-wide statement Phase 3 might make.
4. **Asymmetric-evidence risk.** Because Southend-administered schools have a richer statistical base than Essex-administered schools (see Section 7), Phase 3 must avoid implying the 3 Essex schools are less rigorously run simply because less LA-level statistical data about them survives publicly — that would be an evidence-availability artefact, not an educational finding.
5. **Unread-document risk.** The 7 image-only CSSE PDFs could contain the exact standardisation/candidate-volume figures Phase 3 might want to cite for those years — if Phase 3 needs those specific years, this should be flagged as a targeted follow-up acquisition (e.g., a rendering-capable environment or a manual review) rather than worked around with a secondary or estimated figure.

## 11. Recommendation

**Ready with limitations.**

Phase 3 (educational analysis) may begin on the well-evidenced 2023-2027 window, across all 7 named schools, with the standardisation methodology, current oversubscription criteria, and (for most schools) recent score-cutoff history all resting on independently-verified, multiply-corroborated Official Primary evidence.

Phase 3 must not be opened on the assumption that 2016-2022 coverage is equivalent — it is real but Limited-to-Partial for most schools and most evidence categories, and any Phase 3 output touching that period must carry that caveat explicitly rather than average it away against the stronger recent years.

The two live Level-1 conflicts and the CSSE 7-vs-10-school scope question should be either resolved or explicitly carried forward as open questions in whatever Phase 3 produces — they are not blocking issues, but they are known unknowns that should not quietly disappear.

No further acquisition work is required before Phase 3 may begin, but three low-cost follow-up actions would materially strengthen the corpus if the Founder wants to authorise them before proceeding: (1) a Wayback-Machine-capable pass to attempt recovery of pre-2023 policy text, (2) physical mirroring of the highest-value already-verified sources into this repository's own storage, and (3) a rendering-capable pass at the 7 unread CSSE image PDFs.
