## P0 — Blocks trust in what Angel already claims to have shipped

1. **17-unanswered-question integrity closure.** The scoring mechanism is now proven structurally sound end-to-end (`INCREMENT025_PROGRAMME_COMPLETION_EVIDENCE.md` §14), but the final per-question confirmation for the one real released attempt still requires a Founder-run SQL query (given in full in that section). Until run, the released report's 17-unanswered figure is "mechanism-proven legitimate" but not yet "row-by-row confirmed." Zero engineering work remains — this is purely a Founder action.
2. **Mock composition figures must be read live, never from migration text.** Confirmed twice independently this session (Mathematics Mock 1: migration 147's authored 21-question/56-mark text vs. the live 56-row/56-mark reality; the "NOT APPLIED" header itself, proven unreliable twice before in the existing audit). Any future capacity/readiness claim about Mock content must cite a live query, not a migration file, or must explicitly disclose that it has not been re-verified live.
3. **Zero usage/performance telemetry despite live traffic.** `usage_count = 0` and `avg_success_rate = null` on all 351 practice-eligible rows (confirmed this pass), despite real Mock attempts existing (Mathematics Mock 1: 2 attempts; Reading Mock 1: 5 attempts). Either the write path that should increment these on every Practice/Mock attempt is not firing, or no Practice attempts have actually been served since these columns were introduced — not diagnosed this pass, and it blocks Performance Calibration (Question Factory Stage 12) before it can even begin.

## P1 — Directly contradicts a core product promise already made to families

4. **Preparation Horizon is 0% wired.** A real, correctly-computed engine exists (`preparationClock.ts`/`preparationStage.ts`) but changes nothing about what content or Mock access a learner gets — confirmed unchanged since the existing audit first found this (§8/§24.8/§24.11, ranked there as the single biggest verified gap in the whole audit, a ranking this register does not disturb). Directly contradicts the Founder's own stated requirement that a Year 4 long-runway learner must not drill Mocks prematurely and a late Year 6 entrant must not be forced through unnecessary foundation work. **This is a connection problem, not a build problem** — see `ANGEL_YEAR_4_5_6_COVERAGE_MATRIX.md` §6 for the two exact wiring points.
5. **Sustained practice at any realistic intensity exhausts fresh content in days, not weeks.** Newly quantified against the Founder's own named scenarios (`ANGEL_QUESTION_DEPTH_AND_REPETITION_AUDIT.md` §8): 20-30 questions/day, 5-7 days/week, exhausts the raw Mathematics pool in under 2 weeks and genuine family-diversity in 2-5 days, for every duration from 4 to 26 weeks. Anti-memorisation risk is independently classified HIGH, rising to CRITICAL for a frequent/capable user (unchanged from the existing audit's own §6).
6. **CSSE coverage is 100%; every other advertised pathway is 0%.** New finding this pass: `lib/pathways.ts` presents up to six pathway options to parents (GL, ISEB Common Pre-Test, Bespoke, etc.) with descriptive `recommendedYears` text, but zero live content exists behind any pathway except CSSE, and the marketing metadata is not even wired to the content-selection code (`lib/ali/pathwayEligibility.ts`) — confirmed no shared identifiers. A parent selecting any non-CSSE pathway today receives a product that cannot honestly serve their stated need. This is a genuine content/marketing mismatch requiring either a marketing correction (stop advertising unsupported pathways) or a content investment decision — a Founder call, not an engineering one.
7. **Content-governance foundation exists and is entirely unwired.** Seven real, well-reasoned modules (`inventoryClass.ts`, `structuralSignature.ts`, `antiMemorisationChecks.ts`, `effectiveFreshCapacity.ts`, `englishFamilyModel.ts`, `contentPipeline.ts`, `questionFamilyRegistry.ts`) have zero call sites outside their own files and, in most cases, no test coverage beyond their own unit tests (`ANGEL_QUESTION_SUPPLY_ARCHITECTURE.md` §2). This is the cheapest, lowest-risk item in this entire register — wiring existing, already-correct code, not building anything new — and should be sequenced ahead of any content-volume expansion.
8. **English Reading has no family/repetition-safety concept at all.** Not "unmeasured" — a genuine schema gap (`ANGEL_QUESTION_DEPTH_AND_REPETITION_AUDIT.md` §3). 142 practice-eligible questions cannot be assessed for repetition risk even in principle until `englishFamilyModel.ts`'s reasoning-pattern derivation (Section 7 above) is wired and, ideally, persisted.
9. **Passage-level eligibility lags question-level eligibility.** Only 1/30 English passages is `practice_eligible` (confirmed live, `ANGEL_MOCK_DEPTH_AND_SECURITY_AUDIT.md` §1, citing the existing audit's own §24.2 finding) despite 142 questions being practice-eligible — passage eligibility, not question eligibility, may be the true binding constraint on reachable Reading content. Not yet root-caused.

## P2 — Real, but does not block current product integrity

10. **Question Factory Stage 3 (Candidate) does not exist in any form.** Every content source evaluated in `ANGEL_QUESTION_SUPPLY_ARCHITECTURE.md` (parametric generation, controlled English generation, past-paper ingestion) depends on a genuine not-yet-trusted candidate stage existing first — today, content is either unwritten or already a finished, trusted bank row, with nothing in between. Correctly sequenced last among the "build new" items, since P1 items 4/7/8 are cheaper and higher-leverage.
11. **Past-paper licensing is an open Founder decision**, not an engineering gap (`ANGEL_PAST_PAPER_INGESTION_SPECIFICATION.md`). No ingestion should proceed until this is resolved.
12. **Provenance and quality checks are incomplete.** 11.4% of practice-eligible rows have a null `provenance` field (new finding, `ANGEL_EDUCATIONAL_CONTENT_INVENTORY.md` §7); no automated check exists that a stored `explanation` actually matches its own answer (Question Factory Stage 9); no semantic/conceptual-similarity check exists beyond the structural (non-semantic) signature already in place.
13. **`ali_mock_cycle`'s live role is unverified** — flagged by the Mock depth/security fork as out of its time budget and not covered by the existing capacity audit either (`ANGEL_MOCK_DEPTH_AND_SECURITY_AUDIT.md` §5).
14. **No onboarding/placement diagnostic exists for new or late-entering learners** — carried forward unchanged from the existing audit (§9/§21); the one genuinely new, bounded build this register recommends alongside wiring the Preparation Horizon clock (item 4).
15. **`docs/intelligence/CONTENT_SCALE_FOUNDATION_V1.md` is referenced but does not exist.** `lib/ali/questionFamilyRegistry.ts`'s own docstring cites this file for the full definition of its `VariationStrategy` vocabulary; confirmed absent anywhere in the repository. A small documentation gap, not a functional one, but worth closing before any team member other than the current session relies on that docstring's citation.

## Explicitly NOT a gap (verified this pass, record so it is not re-investigated)

- **The Practice/Mock content firewall.** Genuinely well-engineered, test-proven, and now independently re-confirmed twice more this session: once via this session's own real Reading Mock attempt release (drew exclusively from the frozen manifest, zero Practice-pool leakage), and once via direct RLS inspection (`ali_mock_form`'s anon read policy deliberately dropped by migration 071 — Mock composition is genuinely unreadable outside an admin channel).
- **The retention/spaced-resurfacing mechanism** (`lib/ali/selection.ts` cooldown system). Real, wired, working as designed — the risk it interacts with (thin family pools) is a content-depth gap, not a defect in this mechanism.
- **The scoring pipeline's "unanswered" classification mechanism**, following this session's own Section 14 evidence package — structurally sound, single-sourced from the persisted response, never re-derived downstream.
