# CSSE Examination Blueprint

**Work Package:** ANGEL-CSSE-001 (CSSE Examination Research Programme) — Deliverable 1
**Status:** Documentation only. No implementation. Consolidates an existing frozen source (`docs/intelligence/ASSESSMENT_BRAIN_V1.md`) with new, independently-sourced 2026 research. One material correction is identified and logged (Section 5) — not silently applied to the frozen document.

---

## 1. What this document is

Two things exist that answer "what does the CSSE exam actually look like," and this Blueprint keeps them clearly separated rather than blending them into one undifferentiated claim:

1. **Assessment Brain V1** (`docs/intelligence/ASSESSMENT_BRAIN_V1.md`, frozen 2026-07-20) — built from **17 Accepted CSSE Knowledge Assets**: the actual 2021, 2022, and 2023 English papers, Continuous Writing papers, Mathematics papers, and their marking schemes, plus the CSSE Information Guide. This is primary-source evidence, asset-cited down to the individual observation, and is the more rigorous and specific of the two sources. It is reproduced in Section 2 below, unaltered, with full attribution.
2. **New research performed for this work package** — public information published by the CSSE Consortium itself (csse.org.uk) and cross-checked against three independent 11+ preparation providers, gathered 2026-07-20. This is weaker evidence than (1) — secondary/tertiary sources describing the exam rather than the exam papers themselves — but it is more current, and it surfaces one real, dated structural change Assessment Brain V1's 2021-2023 asset base could not have captured. Reproduced in Sections 3-4, with every claim individually sourced.

**This document does not merge the two into a single unified structure.** Where they agree, that agreement is evidence of stability. Where they disagree, Section 5 states the disagreement plainly and identifies which source is more likely current, without editing the frozen original.

## 2. Established structure (Assessment Brain V1, 2021-2023 papers)

Reproduced from `ASSESSMENT_BRAIN_V1.md` §2, unaltered. Confidence ratings are Assessment Brain's own (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW/INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE, defined in that document's §6).

CSSE candidates sit two tests on the same day — English and Mathematics — each raw-scored, standardised, age-adjusted, and weighted 50/50 into a combined score; no re-marking; no offer below 303 (Observation 1, HIGH).

**English** — a single 70-minute sitting (60 minutes + 10 minutes reading time), internally divided into three timed, separately-scored sections (Observation 2, HIGH):

| Section | Duration | Content | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Comprehension | 30 min (+10 min reading) | Narrative-fiction passage (Observation 6) | HIGH (present) / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE (genre generalises beyond 3 years) |
| Applied Reasoning | 10 min | 5-question letter/word-code puzzle; genre stable, exact mechanic varies yearly (Observation 5) | HIGH (structural) / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE (mechanic) |
| Continuous Writing | 20 min, separate booklet, completable any time within the 60 min | Two prompts: a reflective/discursive question, then a picture-stimulus narrative (Observation 8) | HIGH (format) / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE (topics) |

Comprehension+Reasoning combined section total varies year to year (45 vs 40 marks; the 2022 figure is unextractable from the available marking scheme) — Observation 4, MEDIUM. Comprehension mark schemes explicitly instruct flexible/paraphrase acceptance, not exact-wording matching — Observation 7, HIGH. Continuous Writing's numeric mark total varies (15/15/20 across the three years) — Observation 9, MEDIUM — and a gap between the qualitative marking rubric and the numeric mark total remains open and unresolved — Observation 10, LOW. A Content/SPAG marks split was found on the 2023 Continuous Writing paper only, absent from 2021/2022 — Observation 13, MEDIUM.

**Mathematics** — a single 60-minute, 60-mark paper: **20 numbered questions in 2023, 21 in 2021 and 2022**, most with lettered sub-parts (Observation 3, HIGH) — calculators prohibited, marking is exact-match only with no partial/method credit described in any mark scheme. Content spans arithmetic, algebraic/symbolic reasoning, geometric/spatial reasoning, multi-step word problems, and number theory, confirmed consistently across all three available years (Observation 11, HIGH).

**Full detail** — all 13 Assessment Observations with their individual confidence ratings and Asset ID citations, the complete 13-competency structure, and the complete 27-Question-Type catalogue — lives in `ASSESSMENT_BRAIN_V1.md` itself; this section is a summary extract, not a replacement. See Deliverable 2 (`CSSE_COMPETENCY_TOPIC_MAPPING.md`) and Deliverable 3 (`CSSE_QUESTION_TAXONOMY.md`) for how this work package treats that material.

## 4. Disagreement between sources — resolved, not blended

Two independent sources ([examberrypapers.co.uk](https://examberrypapers.co.uk/exam-information/11-plus-csse/) and [mathsconcierge.co.uk](https://mathsconcierge.co.uk/11-csse-format/)) describe the English Comprehension section as containing **"a mixture of multiple choice and written answer questions,"** while [elevenace.com](https://elevenace.com/csse-essex-11-plus-exam-key-dates-test-format-grammar-schools-guide/) describes the paper as **"Written (not multiple choice)."** This is a direct contradiction between two secondary sources, neither of which is the exam paper itself. Assessment Brain V1's own Observation 7 (HIGH confidence, drawn from the real marking schemes) states comprehension mark schemes "instruct flexible/paraphrase acceptance" — language that only makes sense for written, not multiple-choice, answers. **Section 2's primary-source evidence is treated as authoritative on this point; the "multiple choice" claim in two secondary sources is flagged as likely inaccurate or describing a different/older paper version, not adopted.**

## 5. Identified change requiring a formal correction to Assessment Brain V1 — not applied here

**Finding:** one independent source ([elevenace.com](https://elevenace.com/csse-essex-11-plus-exam-key-dates-test-format-grammar-schools-guide/)) states explicitly: *"With effect from September 2024 (2025 Entry) the English paper does not contain Applied Reasoning questions."*

**Why this matters:** Assessment Brain V1's entire evidence base is the 2021, 2022, and 2023 papers — all three years predate this change and could not have captured it. If accurate, the English paper's Applied Reasoning section (Assessment Brain V1 Observation 2/5, competency AR-01, Question Type QT-AR-01) has been **removed from the live exam for the current and future intake years**, meaning a real, current section of Assessment Brain V1's structure is now historical rather than current.

**Why this is not simply applied as a correction here:** Assessment Brain V1 is explicitly frozen (`ASSESSMENT_BRAIN_V1.md` §10: *"no further educational conclusions, corrections, or additions may be made to this document... without a new, separately-authorised work package... a numbered Correction Log entry in the source document, with full audit trail, consolidated into a new Brain version"*). This finding rests on a single secondary source, not the primary 2024/2025 exam papers themselves (which are not in this repository's Knowledge Asset base and were not independently verified against a second corroborating source in this research pass). Applying a structural correction — removing an entire assessed section, competency, and Question Type — on single-source, secondary evidence would violate the same evidentiary discipline that makes Assessment Brain V1 trustworthy in the first place.

**Recommended next step, for Founder decision, not performed here:** acquire the actual CSSE Guide for 2027 Entry (the real, current primary source referenced in Section 3) and, if it confirms the Applied Reasoning removal, run a formal, separately-authorised correction work package against Assessment Brain V1 — following the exact Correction Log pattern that document's own §10 already establishes, producing Assessment Brain V1.1. This would retire AR-01 and QT-AR-01 from the *current* exam model (they would remain historically documented, not deleted) and would very likely also close Assessment Brain V1's own open Observation 5 (Applied Reasoning mechanic, previously rated INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE) by making the question moot rather than answering it.

## 6. Deliberately not attempted in this document: a full historical "changes by examination year" log

The work package asks for "changes by examination year." Beyond the one 2024 Applied Reasoning removal identified above, this research pass found no further year-by-year change log — none of the four sources consulted provided one, and Assessment Brain V1's own three-year evidence base is a snapshot of 2021-2023, not a change history (where its Observations note year-to-year variation — e.g. Observation 9's 15/15/20 mark totals — that is presented as *variation*, not as an *intentional policy change*, and this document does not reinterpret it as one without evidence). A genuine historical change log would require either the CSSE Consortium's own archived guides for each entry year, or several more years of primary-source papers than the 17 Knowledge Assets currently held. Flagged as an open item, not fabricated.
