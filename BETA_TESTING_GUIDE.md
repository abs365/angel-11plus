# Angel 11+ Beta Testing Guide

## Purpose

This guide helps the founder run structured beta testing with real families.
The goal is learning, not selling. Every interaction is an opportunity to understand
whether Angel 11+ works without founder assistance.

---

## Recruiting Beta Families

### Who to recruit

Target families who:
- Have a child in Year 4, 5 or 6
- Are considering grammar school or independent school entry
- Are not yet using a tutor (fresh perspective is valuable)
- Are comfortable using an app on an iPad or laptop
- Are willing to give honest, candid feedback

Avoid:
- Friends who will only say positive things
- Families in crisis mode (exam in 4 weeks) — too much pressure for beta feedback
- Other founders or product people — their feedback is too product-biased

### Where to find families

1. **Local WhatsApp / Facebook parent groups** — most direct. Post a brief message:
   > "We're testing a new 11+ preparation app and looking for 10 families to try it for free.
   > No experience needed. Just an iPad or laptop and 20 mins/day. Reply here or email me."

2. **School gates** — introduce yourself honestly as a founder testing a product.

3. **LinkedIn** — post as a founder story, not an ad.

4. **Mumsnet** — specifically the 11+ prep boards. Be transparent it's a beta.

5. **Local tutoring groups** — some tutors are open to referring families to tools.

### What to say when recruiting

Keep it honest and low-pressure:

> "I'm building an 11+ prep platform and I need real families to test it.
> It's completely free during beta. You get full access to everything — English, Maths,
> Reasoning, Writing, Mocks, and a Parent Dashboard. All I ask is that you use it for
> at least 2 weeks and tell me honestly what you think."

## Onboarding a Beta Family

### Before they start

1. Send them the link to `/beta` — this page explains everything
2. Tell them to read `/getting-started` — the 5-step parent guide
3. Ask them to register at `/beta-family` — this captures their pathway and email
4. Confirm receipt within 24 hours

### First week check-in (Day 7)

Send a brief email asking:

1. Has your child used it? How many times?
2. Was the Getting Started guide clear?
3. Did they choose a pathway? Which one?
4. What did they try first?
5. Any problems at all — no matter how small?

### Second week check-in (Day 14)

Ask:

1. Are they still using it? What subjects most?
2. Have you checked the Parent Dashboard? What did you think?
3. Have they tried a mock exam?
4. What's missing?
5. Would you recommend it to another parent?

## What to Observe

### The key question

Can a parent and child use Angel 11+ independently — without asking you anything?

### Signals of success

- Family selects a pathway on their first visit
- Parent checks the Parent Dashboard within the first week
- Child has a streak of 3+ days
- Family attempts a mock exam without prompting
- Parent can describe what Angel 11+ does to another person in 30 seconds

### Signals of friction

- Family emails asking "how do I...?" (any question means something wasn't clear)
- Child does only one subject repeatedly (others are confusing or broken)
- Parent says they don't understand the progress scores
- Mock exam is abandoned mid-section
- Family stops after 5 days without explanation

### Watch-list by page

| Page | What to watch for |
|------|------------------|
| `/dashboard` | Does the daily mission make sense? Do they know what to do? |
| `/pathways` | Do they find the right pathway? Do they get confused by the options? |
| `/parent` | Does the parent understand the readiness score? |
| `/mocks` | Do they attempt a mock? Do they understand it's timed? |
| `/getting-started` | Do parents visit this page? How far do they read? |
| `/english/[id]` | Do they write full answers or one-word answers? |

## Feedback That Matters Most

### High-value feedback

| Type | Why it matters |
|------|---------------|
| "I couldn't find X" | Navigation or labelling problem |
| "I didn't understand what X meant" | Language is too complex or jargon-heavy |
| "My child didn't want to go back" | Engagement or motivation issue |
| "The parent dashboard confused me" | Parent experience is broken |
| "We tried a mock and it crashed" | Critical bug, fix immediately |
| "We would pay £X/month for this" | Pricing signal — extremely valuable |
| "We recommended it to another family" | Product-market fit signal |

### Low-value feedback (for now)

- "Can you add [specific exam school]?" — too narrow for beta
- "The colour of X" — cosmetic, Phase 4+
- "I wish it had [big new feature]" — capture, but don't pivot

### How to get honest feedback

Frame it clearly:

> "Pretend I am not involved. Tell me exactly what confused you, what frustrated you,
> and what you would change. The more critical you are, the more useful it is.
> Positive-only feedback doesn't help us build something better."

## Conversation Guide

### 15-minute discovery call script

1. (2 min) **Context**: "Tell me about your child and your 11+ timeline."
2. (3 min) **First impression**: "Walk me through what happened when you first opened Angel 11+."
3. (5 min) **Usage**: "Take me through a typical session. What do they do first? Then what?"
4. (3 min) **Gaps**: "What did you expect to find that wasn't there?"
5. (2 min) **Recommendation**: "Would you recommend this to another parent? Why or why not?"

### Key follow-up probes

- "What did you mean by...?" — always follow up on vague answers
- "Can you show me?" — screen share is gold
- "What did you expect to happen instead?" — more useful than "what went wrong?"
- "If this cost £X, would you pay?" — don't skip this question

## Founder Mindset

### Do
- Stay quiet when they're using the app — let them struggle (that's the data)
- Write down the exact words families use — these become your copy
- Follow up on every piece of feedback within 24 hours
- Fix critical bugs immediately; schedule minor ones

### Don't
- Explain things when they get confused — that means you failed the product test
- Defend design decisions in the call — take notes, review later
- Ask leading questions: "Did you like the dark mode?" → "What did you think of the visual design?"
- Skip families who churned — they're the most valuable conversations

---

## After 10 Families — What to Assess

Answer these questions before moving to 25 families:

1. Can families onboard themselves without founder help?
2. Do parents understand the exam pathway system?
3. Is the Parent Dashboard giving useful signals?
4. Are mock exams completing without bugs?
5. What are the top 3 feature requests?
6. What are the top 3 friction points?
7. What is the retention rate after 2 weeks?
8. Would at least 5 families pay for this product?

If the answer to question 1 is **No** — stop recruiting and fix the product first.
