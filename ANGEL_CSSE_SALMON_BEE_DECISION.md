# English Form B: Salmon versus the bee Timed Section: overlap analysis and recommendation (2026-10-08)

**Recommendation: B. Replace Salmon with a new original informational passage on a different topic and structure.** Differentiating Salmon's questions alone (option A) cannot fix the overlap,
because it sits in the passage prose as well as in the question format. Nothing has been changed or retired; this is a decision for the Founder.

## Evidence (computed from the stored production text; the bee passage is `eng-inc001-bee`, in the live Timed Section)

| Measure | Bee and Salmon | Baseline (bee and Compass Rose, a different genre) |
|---|---|---|
| Passage length | 569 and 571 words | n/a |
| Distinct content words shared | 70 (overlap 0.184) | 32 (overlap 0.071), so **2.6 times higher** |
| Verbatim 5-word runs shared | **10**, forming two 8-word sentence frames | none of note |

**Two near-verbatim sentence frames.**
- Bee: "…one of the most remarkable feats of natural navigation known to scientists…". Salmon: "…one of the most remarkable feats of natural navigation scientists have studied."
- Bee: "What is already clear is that a creature with a brain smaller than a grain of rice is capable of…". Salmon: "What is already clear is that a creature with no map, no memory… is nonetheless capable of…"

**Same argumentative arc.** An animal finds its way home by several senses; a magnetic sense is used; "exactly how… is still being studied"; the closing paragraph names the creature's lack of a map. Both open with the
same puzzle and both end on the same marvelling formula.

**Same assessment structure.**

| Format | Bee (live) | Salmon (reserve) |
|---|---|---|
| Two retrieval questions on a number or name | Q1, Q2 | Q1, Q2 (and Q7) |
| Tick Yes or No, then give reasons from the text (inference, multi-mark) | Q3 | Q3 |
| Four synonyms with item (a) "done for you as an example" | Q5 | Q4 b-e |
| Put four events in order | Q6 | Q5 |
| Meaning of a word in context | Q4 ('remarkable') | Q8 ('imprint') |

**Cross-section leakage.** The word *remarkable* is the target of bee Q4 and the **worked example given** in Salmon's synonym questions. A child who sits both would meet it as an answered example in one and a question in the other.

**Why this matters for Form B.** Form B is meant to measure comprehension independently of Form A and the Timed Section. A twin lowers that independence (familiar template, primed topic and phrasing) and would
let a child gain from the earlier timed passage rather than from reading skill. It also breaks the plan's own rule: do not use a structural twin merely to fill Form B.

Other reserve passages do not fill the gap cheaply: *Pepper's Breakfast* is narrative (Form B already has a narrative passage) and short; *The Fossil Hunter of Lyme Regis* is informational but carries only 6 items and
9 marks (7 now manually marked), so it would need 5 more items and still lacks synonym, sequencing and multi-select material. It is better kept for Form C.

## Replacement brief (for approval before any authoring)

- **Passage:** original, informational, British English, about 550 to 600 words, moderate difficulty, age-appropriate for 10 to 11 year olds, **not about animal navigation or senses**, with a **different organising
  pattern** from bee and Salmon (for example cause and effect, problem and solution, or a process over time), and **no formulaic closer** of the "What is already clear is that…" kind. Facts must be ones a validator can
  verify from a reliable source, and each factual claim is listed for the validator.
- **Items required so Form B stays at 24 questions and 39 marks:** 11 items, 18 marks, with the skill profile the Compass Rose half cannot supply: retrieval x3 (1 mark each), inference with evidence x1 (3 marks), meaning
  in context x1 (2 marks, manually marked), synonyms x4 (1 mark each, with a **different frame** from the bee's), sequencing or structure x1 (4 marks, in a **different format** from "order four events"), and
  writer's choice and effect x1 (2 marks).
- **Governance:** sealed `authentic_assessment_candidate` rows, original content only, marking-contract tests, independent human review before any promotion, nothing applied without your instruction. Ordered-list and
  hedged-answer observations from the verification record are designed out (explicit "one item per line" wording; accepted sets that do not contain a lone wrong-but-adjacent answer).

If you prefer A, say so; I would then rewrite the passage prose as well (otherwise the overlap remains) and still need the same independent review.
