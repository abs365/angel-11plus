Answers were checked against the text: twenty minutes; church bell tower; the bell-shaped weathervane over the sundial; the wooden frame the old market bell hung from; the quotation for Grace's approach is verbatim in the passage; Q3's "each explained half a clue convincingly" is verbatim. Strong reasoning passage and the right "hard / challenge" anchor. Two items to tighten: (a) Q5 asks for the order the passage *describes* the four characters investigating, but the text says "meanwhile" and Grace "had gone" to the noticeboard, so the instruction should say "the order the passage first mentions them"; (b) Q2(b) has Casey believing the *sundial* is the answer, while the question asks what Casey took "the bell" to mean (the weathervane), which is only implicit.
- **Salmon.** Facts as I know them (not a primary-source check; the reviewer must verify): Hasler and Wisby proposed scent imprinting in 1951; magnetic imprinting at the sea entry point is presented with suitable hedging. Question answers match the text (1951; magnetic field; scent too diluted at sea; the ordering item). Not a copy and not unsafe, but a learner who has met both gets a familiar frame. Recommendation: acceptable for Form B, with the Timed Section swapped or retired before a learner takes both in one preparation cycle.
- **Fossil Hunter (Anning).** The historical claims are consistent with the commonly cited record as I know it (1799 birth, father's trade and 1810 death, the 1811-12 ichthyosaur, the 1823 plesiosaur, Cuvier's doubt, the landslide that killed Tray, women kept out of the Geological Society until long after her death, 1847 death, the doubtful tongue-twister, the 2010 Royal Society list). That is from my own knowledge, **not a primary-source check**; the human reviewer must verify each date before any use. Good genre variety. **Marking defect (below).**

A small logic slip: Pepper's bowl "from the night before" is still full, yet Ella says she fed her "this morning", so the old bowl would have been replaced. Short (416 words), so it suits an easier section or the diagnostic more than a full-paper passage.
- **Two Different Projects.** Strong inference and emotion material. **Sensitivity for the Founder:** Ben's mother is "back at the hospital" for check-ups (reassured as fine). Mild, but an exam setting should be a deliberate decision. **Marking defect (below).**
