## 1. Executive Summary

Assessment Brain V1.0 consolidates three previously-Accepted Intelligence Frameworks — AEP-002 (assessment structure), AEP-003 (competencies), and AEP-004 (question types) — into a single, internally-consistent, fully cross-referenced reference, built from the 17 Accepted CSSE Knowledge Assets and no other source.

**This is a consolidation, not new analysis.** Per this work package's mission, no new educational conclusion was introduced. Six corrections were applied, all of them findings AEP-004 had already discovered during its own necessary close reading of the primary assets but was not itself authorised to act on:

1. AEP-002 Observation 3's Mathematics question count was factually wrong ("12-question paper") and is corrected to the true count (20 in 2023, 21 in 2021/2022), verified directly from each paper's own "END OF TEST" line.
2. AEP-002 Observation 11's confidence is upgraded MEDIUM→HIGH, and its "one of three years reviewed" limitation is resolved, since AEP-004 completed the full three-year depth review AEP-002 originally lacked.
3. A new AEP-002 Observation 13 records a Content/SPAG marks split found on the 2023 Continuous Writing paper, absent from 2021/2022 — this narrows, but does not resolve, Observation 10's open rubric-vs-marks gap.
4. AEP-003 competencies MR-02, MR-03, and MR-04 are upgraded LOW/EMC-2 → HIGH/EMC-4, the direct consequence of correction 2 above (these competencies were explicitly capped by the same "2023-only" limitation that no longer applies).
5. **MR-05 and MR-01 were explicitly considered for the same upgrade and rejected.** AEP-004 found partial, not full, multi-year support for MR-05, and did not recommend a change to either MR-01 or MR-05 in its own Readiness Assessment. Upgrading them here would exceed this work package's mandate to consolidate only findings already identified in prior work packages — both remain at their original ratings, flagged in Section 8 for a possible future explicit review rather than changed now.
6. Every correction is logged with full audit trail directly inside AEP-002 and AEP-003 (each now carries its own Correction Log section), not only here.

**Scope:** CSSE only. GL, CEM, and ISEB are out of scope for this Brain, matching the scope of all four underlying work packages.

**Headline counts:** 17 Accepted Knowledge Assets → 13 Assessment Observations → 13 Competencies across 4 domains → 27 Question Types across 4 assessment components. Full detail remains in the three source documents (`CSSE_ASSESSMENT_INTELLIGENCE_FRAMEWORK.md`, `CSSE_COMPETENCY_FRAMEWORK.md`, `CSSE_QUESTION_INTELLIGENCE_FRAMEWORK.md`); this Brain is the cross-referenced index and canonical entry point, not a replacement for their prose.

## 2. Assessment Structure

*(Source: AEP-002, as corrected under this consolidation. Full prose and evidence citations in `CSSE_ASSESSMENT_INTELLIGENCE_FRAMEWORK.md`.)*

CSSE candidates sit two tests on the same day — English and Mathematics — each raw-scored, standardised, age-adjusted, and weighted 50/50 into a combined score; no re-marking; no offer below 303 (Observation 1).

**English** is a single 70-minute sitting (60 minutes + 10 minutes reading time) internally divided into three timed, separately-scored sections (Observation 2):

| Section | Duration | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Comprehension | 30 min (+10 min reading) | Questions on a narrative-fiction passage (Observation 6) |
| Applied Reasoning | 10 min | 5-question letter/word-code puzzle, genre stable, mechanic varies yearly (Observation 5) |
| Continuous Writing | 20 min (separate booklet, completable any time in the 60 min) | Two prompts: a reflective/discursive question, then a picture-stimulus narrative (Observation 8; format sub-pattern per AEP-004) |

**Mathematics** is a single 60-minute, 60-mark paper — **20 numbered questions in 2023, 21 in 2021 and 2022**, most with lettered sub-parts (Observation 3, corrected under this consolidation) — calculators prohibited, marking is exact-match only with no partial/method credit described in any mark scheme. Content spans arithmetic, algebraic/symbolic reasoning, geometric/spatial reasoning, multi-step word problems, and number theory, **confirmed consistently across all three available years** (Observation 11, upgraded under this consolidation).

## 6. Evidence Confidence

Scale reused unchanged from AEP-002 §6 (not redefined here, per this repository's "define once, reuse" discipline):

- **HIGH** — directly and explicitly stated in one or more Accepted assets, consistent across all available years, no material limitation.
- **MEDIUM** — directly evidenced but incomplete coverage, or a single-source/uncorroborated caveat.
- **LOW** — evidenced only as an unresolved gap or tension, not a positive finding.
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE** — a structural pattern is evidenced but cannot support a predictive/generalising claim beyond it.

**Distribution across the 13 Assessment Observations (post-correction):** 8 HIGH (1, 2, 3, 5-structural, 6-present, 7, 8-format, 11), 4 MEDIUM (4, 9, 12, 13), 1 LOW (10). *(Note: Observations 5, 6, 8 each carry a second, INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE rating for their non-generalisable component, per AEP-002 §6 — counted once above by their structural rating to avoid double-counting.)*

## 7. Evidence Maturity

Scale reused unchanged from AEP-003 §3(4):

- **EMC-4 (Established):** consistent evidence across all available years, HIGH-confidence observation, no material limitation.
- **EMC-3 (Substantiated):** directly evidenced but MEDIUM-confidence, or HIGH-confidence with a partial-year-coverage limitation.
- **EMC-2 (Indicative):** single-year or single-instance evidence.
- **EMC-1 (Provisional):** named but boundaries/scoring genuinely unresolved by an explicit evidence gap.

**Distribution across the 13 Competencies (post-correction):** 4 EMC-4 (MR-02, MR-03, MR-04, MR-06), 5 EMC-3 (RC-01, RC-02, MR-01, WC-01, and AR-01's abstract form), 3 EMC-2 (RC-03, RC-04, MR-05), 2 EMC-1 (AR-01's concrete mechanic, WC-02). *(AR-01 straddles two EMC levels by design, per AEP-003 Principle 2 — its abstract form is counted in the EMC-3 band, its concrete mechanic separately in the EMC-1 band, so the four bands sum to 14 slots across 13 distinct competencies.)*

Before this consolidation, the distribution was 1 EMC-4 (MR-06), 4 EMC-3 (RC-01, RC-02, MR-01, WC-01 + AR-01 abstract), 6 EMC-2 (RC-03, RC-04, MR-02, MR-03, MR-04, MR-05), 2 EMC-1. The shift (3 competencies moving EMC-2→EMC-4) is the direct, sole effect of this work package's three approved corrections.

## 8. Known Limitations

Consolidated from all three source frameworks; nothing here is new to this Brain — each item traces to an existing Observation, Competency, or Question Type limitation.

- **Structural, still open:** Observation 10 / WC-02's rubric-vs-numeric-marks gap (narrowed by new Observation 13, not resolved). Observation 4's 2022 English combined mark total is genuinely unextractable by this evidence base's tooling. Observation 5 / AR-01's Applied Reasoning mechanic and Observation 6's Comprehension genre coverage cannot be generalised beyond three data points. Observation 12's format-distinctiveness claim is single-source and unverifiable against commercial material not held in this Knowledge Domain.
- **Single-instance competencies, still open:** RC-03, RC-04, MR-05 rest on one observed instance each (three, not six, after this consolidation's corrections — Section 3).
- **Explicitly considered, not changed:** MR-01's EMC-3 cap and MR-05's EMC-2 rating share the same class of limitation as the three competencies upgraded in this consolidation, but neither was named by AEP-004 as ready for revisit, and upgrading them here would exceed this work package's "consolidate only what was already discovered" mandate. Flagged for a possible future explicit review, not resolved now.
- **Competency-model gaps AEP-004 identified, still open:** two recurring Mathematics demands — unit/measurement conversion (QT-MR-03) and data/chart/table reading (QT-MR-09) — have no dedicated AEP-003 competency and are mapped to the closest existing one (MR-01) with a documented imperfect fit. Resolving this would mean inventing a new competency, which is new educational conclusion-making outside every framework's authorised scope to date, including this one.
- **Question Type-level, still open:** 3 Question Types remain single-instance (QT-RC-03, QT-RC-06, QT-RC-09); several remain 2/3-year (QT-RC-04, QT-RC-05, QT-RC-07, QT-RC-08, QT-RC-10, QT-MR-02, QT-MR-13); QT-MR-11's property-satisfying-search sub-format is single-year even though its judgement sub-format is 3/3.
- **Scope boundary, unchanged across all four work packages:** CSSE only. No conclusions here extend to GL, CEM, or ISEB, which have their own separately-documented, lesser-severity mismatches (per AEP-001) not addressed by this Brain.
