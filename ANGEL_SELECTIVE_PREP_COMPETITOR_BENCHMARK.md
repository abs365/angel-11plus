# ANGEL 11+ — Selective-School Preparation Competitor Benchmark

**Status:** External market research only. No Angel product code, database, or content was touched to produce this document. No competitor question content is reproduced anywhere below — only publicly disclosed capability, format and pricing claims, each cited to its source.

**Access date for every citation below, unless stated otherwise:** 2026-09-05.

**Evidence classes used throughout:** **[Self]** = the provider's own marketing/product page (may be biased upward). **[Official]** = the exam board/consortium's own page (CSSE, GL Assessment, ISEB) — authoritative on format, not a commercial claim. **[Independent]** = a third party (forum, review site) with no commercial stake in the provider's success.

---

## 1. Atom Learning

**What it is [Self]:** A subscription 11+/entrance-exam preparation platform. (https://www.atomlearning.com/11-plus, accessed 2026-09-05)

**Subjects / pathways [Self]:** English (reading comprehension, spelling/punctuation/grammar), Maths, Verbal Reasoning, Non-Verbal Reasoning, Science. Explicitly targets GL Assessment (multiple-choice, OMR), independent-school-specific formats, and CSSE (named directly on their own page as a distinct regional pathway). (Same source.)

**Disclosed volume [Self, inconsistent across pages — reported honestly rather than resolved]:** One third-party summary blog cites "over 90,000" questions (https://www.myengineeringbuddy.com/blog/atom-learning-reviews-alternatives-pricing-offerings/, accessed 2026-09-05) — this is **not** Atom's own primary marketing page and should be treated as an unverified secondary claim. Atom's own `/11-plus` page discloses a different, more specific mix: **3,000+ flashcards, 2,000+ help-sheets, 600+ videos**, plus practice papers "for hundreds of grammar and independent schools" (https://www.atomlearning.com/11-plus, accessed 2026-09-05). These are not the same unit of measurement (flashcards/help-sheets ≠ discrete practice questions), so **no single reliable "total question count" can be honestly quoted for Atom** from public sources — this itself is a useful finding: even a well-funded competitor does not publish an audit-grade content count.

**Adaptive practice [Self]:** Yes — described as an algorithm that "adjusts the level of questions to suit how your child learns," personalised further by selected target school. (https://www.atomlearning.com/11-plus)

**Mock exams [Self]:** Online and printable, described as "unlimited" with "new questions each time," instant marking and explanations.

**Explanations [Self]:** "Instant, exam-aligned feedback" with detailed explanations after mocks.

**Parent reporting [Self]:** Standardised Age Score (SAS), performance by subject/topic, comparison to "successful applicants."

**Anti-repetition [Self, explicit claim]:** "Each test has new questions, so they can keep improving without seeing the same content again."

**Pricing [Self, two sources disagree on exact figures — range quoted]:** Three tiers, approximately Core/EMS £32–£40/month, Exam Prep £48–£60/month, Exam Prep Plus £56–£70/month; ~20% discount for annual billing. (https://www.atomlearning.com/11-plus and https://www.myengineeringbuddy.com/blog/atom-learning-reviews-alternatives-pricing-offerings/, both accessed 2026-09-05)

**Independent-source strengths/weaknesses [Independent — Mumsnet/forum, not Atom's own claims]:**
- Recurring parent complaint: **Atom's own practice/mock scores read materially higher than real exam performance** — several parents report needing to subtract 7–10 marks from Atom's SAS to get a "real-life" comparable figure, and describe scores as "veering wildly" with little relation to actual ability. (Mumsnet threads, aggregated via search 2026-09-05: https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/private-school/5503048-what-atom-test-scores-were-you-seeing-7-to-9-months-out, https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/secondary/4541426-atome-learning-for-11)
- Recurring complaint: English questions "poorly worded and ambiguous" in places; Maths difficulty escalation sometimes injects GCSE-level content inappropriate for 11+ age range. (11 Plus Exams Forum thread, https://www.elevenplusexams.co.uk/forum/11plus/viewtopic.php?t=66197, accessed 2026-09-05)
- Consensus view: Atom alone is **not** considered sufficient for the most academically selective independent schools that set their own bespoke written papers, since Atom's format is online/multiple-choice. (11plusapp.co.uk comparison blog, independent commercial competitor, treat as directionally useful but not neutral: https://11plusapp.co.uk/blog/atom-learning-alternative.html)

**CSSE-specific note [Official]:** CSSE's own website references "information about Atom Learning preparation materials (2025)" alongside its official familiarisation guidance — i.e. CSSE itself points families toward Atom, which is a meaningful competitive/positioning fact for Angel (Atom has an implicit CSSE-adjacency Angel does not yet have evidence of). (https://csse.org.uk/examination/, accessed 2026-09-05)

## 2. Bond 11+ (Bond Online, published by Oxford/HarperCollins imprint)

**What it is [Self]:** A long-established 11+ book publisher with an added online subscription product, "Bond Online." (https://www.bond11plus.co.uk/bond-online, accessed 2026-09-05)

**Subjects [Self]:** Maths, Verbal Reasoning, Non-Verbal Reasoning, English.

**Pathways [Self]:** GL- and CEM-style assessments (base tiers); **ISEB Common Pre-Tests specifically** at the Premium Plus tier — and Bond Online Premium Plus is stated to be **"the only ISEB-endorsed test practice platform"** for this exam type (https://www.iseb.co.uk/product/bond-online-premium-plus/, accessed 2026-09-05 — this is a claim repeated on both Bond's and a third-party [ISEB-affiliated retail] page, not independently verified against ISEB's own primary site, but the ISEB-branded product page itself is a fairly strong signal it is a genuine, sanctioned relationship).

**Disclosed volume [Self — two sources disagree materially]:** A search-aggregated summary states "20,000+ questions" for ISEB Pre-Test practice (https://www.century.tech/bond-online-premium-plus-iseb/, accessed 2026-09-05). Bond's own `/bond-online` page states Premium Plus adds "over 2,000 additional questions" specific to the ISEB framework (https://www.bond11plus.co.uk/bond-online, accessed 2026-09-05). **This is a 10x discrepancy between two sources describing the same product** — reported honestly rather than resolved, and itself evidence that competitor-disclosed volume figures are not reliable enough to benchmark against without independent verification.

**Mock exams [Self]:** "Full-length timed mock tests" across all tiers; Premium Plus states 8 mock exams/month in one secondary source (https://www.century.tech/bond-online-premium-plus-iseb/) — not confirmed on Bond's own primary page, which only says "dedicated mock tests" without a monthly count.

**Adaptive practice [Self]:** Premium tier "AI"-branded personalisation, described as adapting to performance and recommending next-best study areas.

**Explanations [Self]:** Video/guidance "learning nuggets" in Premium tiers; "instant marking and reports" in the base tier.

**Parent reporting [Self]:** Progress reports in all tiers; real-time dashboard in Premium tiers.

**Anti-repetition:** Not disclosed on any page reviewed.

**Pricing [Self]:** Bond Online (base) ~£7.50/month; Premium ~£20.66/month (£247.92/yr); Premium Plus ~£28.99/month (£347.92/yr). Practice books remain separate purchases (~£7–£13 each). (https://www.bond11plus.co.uk/bond-online, accessed 2026-09-05)

## 3. BOFA ("Test Teach Retest" — bofa11plus.com / Planet BOFA)

**Verification note:** confirmed as a real, established UK provider (not found to be a misnomer) — operates multiple branded portals (bofa11plus.com, bofaks2.co.uk, london-11plus.co.uk, planetbofa.co.uk), which itself is a slightly confusing multi-brand footprint worth noting as a UX contrast point against Angel's single coherent product surface.

**What it is [Self]:** "Fully automated" AI-branded adaptive testing platform built around a 3-step "Test → Teach → Retest" formative-learning cycle. (https://www.bofa11plus.com/, accessed 2026-09-05)

**Subjects [Self]:** English, Maths, Verbal Reasoning, Non-Verbal Reasoning (11+ product); a separate BOFAMaths product exists for GCSE/IGCSE.

**Pathways [Self]:** 11+ (multiple age entry points, 8+/12+), ISEB and Consortium exams, London Consortium, and school-run mock tests. Notably claims **56% of 2025 Eton College scholarship winners were BOFA users** — a strong, specific, checkable-in-principle claim (not independently verified here; treat as [Self] until corroborated).

**Disclosed volume:** Not disclosed on the pages reviewed.

**Adaptive practice [Self]:** Explicit "move up or down in difficulty depending on performance," with starting difficulty allocated by age and exam date — i.e. a genuinely adaptive item-selection model, not just a fixed curriculum sequence.

**Anti-repetition [Self, explicit and strongly worded]:** **"They'll never come across the same question twice."** This is the single most direct, unambiguous anti-repetition claim found across all providers researched — worth treating as the benchmark claim Angel's own exposure-control design should be measured against, even though it cannot be independently verified from the public page alone.

**Explanations [Self]:** Simple, confidence-building explanation style described.

**Parent reporting [Self]:** Subject-level reports with cohort comparison ("historical and existing cohorts").

**Pricing:** Not shown on the landing page reviewed; directed to a separate shop.

## 4. Examberry

**What it is [Self]:** Primarily a **tuition and practice-paper publisher** (PDF/printed), with a secondary "online practice tests" and separate "mock exam" product (mocks.examberry.com) for specific named schools/regions (Sutton, Tiffin, Slough). (https://examberrypapers.co.uk/, https://mocks.examberry.com/, accessed 2026-09-05)

**Subjects [Self]:** Maths, English, Verbal Reasoning, Non-Verbal Reasoning, and CEM-style content.

**Disclosed volume:** Not a question-bank product in the Atom/Bond sense — sold as discrete papers/bundles (e.g. "11+ QE Boys" bundle) rather than a subscription pool, so "volume" isn't the right comparison axis for this provider.

**Adaptive practice:** No evidence found of an adaptive/personalised digital engine — this is a paper-and-tuition-first business, not a software platform. This is a meaningful category distinction: Examberry competes on human tuition quality and paper fidelity to real exam style, not technology.

**Credible strength [Self, self-reported outcome stat, not independently audited]:** Claims 85–90% of tuition-course pupils receive offers "at schools of their choice" — an outcome claim rather than a content-volume claim.

**Pricing [Self]:** Bundle pricing (e.g. £185–£275 per bundle), tiered discounts for larger orders. Not a monthly-subscription model like Atom/Bond/BOFA.

## 5. 11 Plus Lifeline (RSL Educational)

**What it is [Official/Self]:** A subscription drip-feed of original weekly practice papers plus optional personal marking, run by RSL Educational. (https://www.rsleducational.co.uk/11pluslifeline/, accessed 2026-09-05)

**Subjects [Self]:** Written/multiple-choice Comprehension, Creative & Essay Writing, Maths, Verbal/Non-Verbal Reasoning (including 3D/spatial reasoning).

**Format [Self]:** At least one new paper per week via a member portal; papers are explicitly stated as "never duplicated from RSL Educational books" — a content-freshness claim, though only relative to their own separately-sold books, not a broader anti-repetition system.

**Adaptive practice:** **None found.** No personalisation or difficulty-adjustment pathway is described anywhere on the provider's own page — this is a fixed weekly curriculum, not adaptive software.

**Mock exams:** Not offered as timed full mocks — individual weekly papers only.

**Explanations/marking [Self]:** All tiers include worked solutions; Platinum/Super Platinum tiers add human personal marking feedback (1–2 hours/month).

**Parent reporting:** None described — no dashboard or structured progress tracking found.

**Pricing [Self]:** Bronze £9/mo (1 paper/week), Silver £15/mo (2 papers/week + reasoning resources), Platinum £70/mo (adds ~1hr/mo human marking + up to 2 books/courses), Super Platinum £120/mo (2hrs/mo marking + up to 18 books/4 courses).

## 6. CGP 11+ (CGP Books)

**What it is [Self, via search-aggregated summary — CGP's own product page returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch and could not be independently re-verified in this research pass]:** A long-established UK study-guide publisher's monthly online subscription add-on to its physical book range. (https://www.cgpbooks.co.uk/11-plus-13-plus-books/11/11-plus-online-subscriptions/9plusme1-11-online-monthly-subscription — fetch blocked 2026-09-05; summarised via search index only, flagged as lower-confidence than directly-fetched sources in this document)

**Subjects/pathways [Self]:** "Unlimited practice" across "every subject and test provider," explicitly naming GL and CEM; separate free online resources also exist for CEM (Durham University) content specifically.

**Disclosed volume [Self]:** "Thousands of realistic questions," no specific count.

**Format:** Mix of full mock tests and short "10-question" topic drills — a deliberately granular practice unit smaller than a full mock, worth noting as a UX pattern.

**Parent reporting [Self]:** "Progress stats" showing strengths/weaknesses by topic.

**Adaptive practice:** Not claimed — CGP's online product is a large fixed/rotating question pool with topic filtering, not an adaptive-difficulty engine, as far as could be determined without a confirmed primary-source fetch.

**Pricing [Self]:** Monthly subscription with a 30% annual discount; exact monthly figure not confirmed in this pass (source blocked).

**Also relevant:** CGP publishes free online CEM/GL practice papers outside the subscription product, and physical books ship with a free "Online Edition" access code — a hybrid print+digital model distinct from every purely-digital competitor above.

## 7. GL Assessment (the exam board / publisher itself)

**What it is [Official]:** GL Assessment is the exam board, not a coaching platform — it is one of the two dominant 11+ test formats (alongside CEM-style) that every commercial competitor above is preparing children *for*. (https://11plus.gl-assessment.co.uk/pages/free-materials, accessed 2026-09-05)

**Official free materials [Official]:** "10 hours of free familiarisation materials" as downloadable PDFs, covering Verbal Reasoning, Non-Verbal Reasoning, English, Maths, Verbal Skills, and a combined NVR+Maths pack.

**Paid materials [Official]:** Practice-paper packs — three books per subject area plus a bundled set; each book contains three question papers, answer sheets, and parent guidance. Specific pricing not disclosed on the page reviewed.

**Adaptive practice / mocks / parent reporting:** **None** — GL Assessment's own consumer-facing page offers no adaptive engine, no automated mock scoring, and no parent dashboard. This confirms the expected structural fact: the exam board sets the format and licenses/sells raw familiarisation content; every adaptive/reporting/mock-automation feature seen elsewhere in this document is a third-party value-add built on top of (not by) the exam board.

## 8. CSSE — Consortium of Selective Schools in Essex (official)

**What it is [Official]:** The examining consortium itself for the Essex grammar-school pathway Angel already targets. (https://csse.org.uk/, https://csse.org.uk/examination/, accessed 2026-09-05)

**Official materials [Official]:** Free downloadable familiarisation papers (Maths, English) plus a paid "Order Practice Packs" online order form; a "PREPARING FOR 11+" guidance document (dated 2020 per the page's own reference); and — notably — the CSSE site itself references **"information about Atom Learning preparation materials (2025)"**, i.e. CSSE points prospective families toward a named commercial competitor.

**Format confirmation [Official, independently corroborates Angel's own internal history]:** Independent search results confirm that **CSSE's English paper stopped containing Applied Reasoning questions from September 2024 (2025 entry) onward** — this matches and independently corroborates this repository's own prior internal finding (Decision 58–61, "Applied Reasoning reclassified," referenced in this session's memory of past CSSE Completion Programme work). This is a genuine external confirmation that Angel's own content-classification correction was aligned with the real, current exam specification, not merely an internal assumption.

**Adaptive practice / mocks / parent reporting:** None offered directly by CSSE (as expected — CSSE is the examining body, not a prep platform).

**Direct competitive implication for Angel:** Angel is the only reviewed product built *specifically and solely* around the CSSE pathway as a first-class target (every other competitor treats CSSE, if at all, as one region among several GL-style pathways). This is a genuine, evidenced differentiation opportunity — not previously stated as fact anywhere in this document until now, and not to be confused with a claim that Angel currently has deep CSSE-specific content depth (that is a separate, unresolved question addressed in the sibling content-inventory document).

## 9. ISEB (Independent Schools Examinations Board) — Common Pre-Tests (official)

**What it is [Official]:** The ISEB Common Pre-Tests are four multiple-choice, **genuinely adaptive**, computer-based tests (English, Maths, Verbal Reasoning, Non-Verbal Reasoning), ~2 hours 15 minutes total, used by many leading independent schools for Year 7 (or delayed Year 9) entry. (https://www.iseb.co.uk/assessments/common-pre-tests/for-families/, https://www.iseb.co.uk/families-hub/pre-tests-admissions-toolkit/preparing-for-the-pre-tests/iseb-pre-tests-test-framework/, accessed 2026-09-05)

**How the adaptive format actually works [Official — precise and directly useful for Angel's own architecture design]:** After each response, the system assesses ability and selects the next question at the most appropriate difficulty; different pupils see a different number of questions and a genuinely different path through the item pool; **pupils cannot go back and change previous answers**, which is a deliberate psychometric-integrity design choice worth Angel noting for any future "real mock" fidelity work.

**Official familiarisation [Official]:** ISEB itself provides one official online familiarisation test, explicitly intended to be used **once, close to the real test date**, purely to reduce format-unfamiliarity nerves — explicitly **not** positioned as revision material. This is a clean, board-sanctioned example of the difference between "familiarisation" (format exposure) and "practice" (skill-building), a distinction Angel's own mock/practice separation should be able to defend in identical terms.

**Endorsed commercial partner [cross-referenced from Section 2]:** Bond Online Premium Plus is marketed as the (a) only ISEB-endorsed practice platform for this exam type — i.e. ISEB has one sanctioned commercial relationship, and it is not with Atom, BOFA, or CGP.

## 10. Synthesis — what should Angel actually compete on?

**Does the evidence gathered here actually support an ASSESS→DIAGNOSE→TEACH→PRACTISE→ADAPT→REASSESS→PROVE-PROGRESS positioning, or something else?**

The evidence supports it, but with an important qualification: **almost every competitor above already claims some version of "adaptive" and "progress reporting"** (Atom, Bond, BOFA, and — officially — the ISEB Pre-Test format itself are all adaptive in the genuine psychometric sense). So "we are adaptive" is not, by itself, a differentiator — it is close to table stakes among the digital-platform competitors (CGP, Examberry papers, and 11 Plus Lifeline are the exceptions, being fixed-curriculum/paper-first businesses). Angel cannot win on the mere existence of adaptivity; it can only win on **fidelity and honesty of that loop**, for three concrete, evidenced reasons:

1. **Competitors' own users report the loop doesn't hold up under scrutiny.** The single clearest, most concrete, independently-sourced weakness found in this entire research pass is Atom Learning's own parent community reporting that its adaptive scores materially diverge from real exam performance (needing manual correction of 7–10 marks). This is precisely the failure mode of an ASSESS→...→PROVE-PROGRESS claim made without the evidentiary rigour to back it — and it is a direct, checkable contrast point for Angel **if and only if Angel's own equivalent claim (its scoring/analysis/reporting pipeline) is actually held to a higher evidentiary standard.** This is not automatically true of Angel merely by intention; it must be demonstrated with the same evidence discipline as this whole assignment demands. (See the sibling `ANGEL_EDUCATIONAL_CONTENT_INVENTORY.md` / 17-unanswered-question evidence work in this same programme for exactly that kind of scrutiny being applied to Angel's own pipeline.)

2. **No competitor researched here discloses an audit-grade content-depth figure.** Every volume claim found (Atom's "90,000" vs. its own "3,000 flashcards," Bond's "20,000+" vs. its own "2,000 additional") is internally inconsistent or unverifiable. If Angel can produce and stand behind a genuinely audited, honestly-caveated content-depth figure (distinguishing database rows from real educational families, exactly as this whole assignment already demands of Angel internally), that alone is a defensible, differentiated claim almost no competitor here could currently make about themselves.

3. **CSSE-specific depth is a real, evidenced, currently-uncontested gap.** No competitor researched treats CSSE as more than a secondary regional variant of a GL-style test; CSSE's own site currently points families toward Atom (a generalist), not a CSSE specialist. If Angel's actual CSSE-specific content depth is genuinely strong (a question this document does not answer — see the content-inventory document), "the CSSE specialist, not a generalist adapted to CSSE" is an available, currently-empty competitive position.

**Recommendation:** Do not lead marketing or product design on "we are adaptive" (undifferentiated) or "we have the most questions" (unverifiable even for competitors, and explicitly rejected as a goal by the Founder). Lead on **provable diagnostic honesty** (a report that says "no secure strengths" when that's true, rather than manufacturing false comparators — which Angel has already demonstrably built into its own report-copy standard this session) and **CSSE-specific depth**, both of which are currently either uncontested or actively where a leading competitor (Atom) is shown, by its own users, to be weak.
