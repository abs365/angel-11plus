## E. Continuous Writing requirement

**Confirmed real, current, and compulsory** (a defunct component would not have a live official mark scheme actively hosted and linked from the current site).

- **Format**: 2 tasks — Q1 always reflective/discursive (the child's own experience or opinion), Q2 always a picture-stimulus narrative ("write a story based on the picture below") — stable across all 3 primary-source years read. No evidence of task choice; both appear to be required, marked as one piece of work.
- **Time**: no separate enforced clock — ~20 minutes is CSSE's own *suggested* allocation within the single 60-minute English working time, not a separately timed phase (unlike reading time, which is officially separate).
- **Length**: minimum 6 sentences, stable 3/3 years.
- **Marks**: Writing itself scored 15 marks (2021, 2022) → 20 marks (2023, with a Content(15)/SPAG(5) split that year specifically). The *total paper* split against Comprehension remains an open conflict (§C).
- **Marking**: double-marked plus moderation — a real process Angel structurally cannot replicate with a single automated pass (disclosed, not solved, by the repo's own prior research).
- **Criteria — OFFICIAL, read directly from CSSE's own hosted rubric**: Ideas, Vocabulary (incl. spelling), Grammar, Structure, Punctuation — a genuine 4-band descriptor scale. **Grammar has a populated descriptor for Band 4 only** — Bands 1-3 have no official text. This is CSSE's own evidence gap, not Angel's.
- **Spelling/punctuation/grammar/vocabulary/organisation/relevance**: all covered, exactly as named, within the 5-criterion rubric above — no finer official breakdown exists.
- **Handwriting/presentation**: no official criterion found (also not applicable to Angel's digital format).
